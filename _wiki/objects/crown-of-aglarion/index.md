#Object 

The crown of the royal family of <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/aglarion/">Aglarion</a>. Currently in possession of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a>.

During the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/kidnapping-of-queen-lyrencia/">Kidnapping of Queen Lyrencia</a>, it was presumably enchanted with a <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/blessing-of-plenty/">Blessing of Plenty</a> by <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-dasmag/">King Dasmag</a>.

But following <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-coronation-of-aphina/">The Coronation of Aphina</a>, it instead is apparently the focal point of <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/the-curse/">The Curse</a>.

When Aphina unleashed the curse, <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Bone-like Protrusions</span> dug into her skin, causing <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Blue Vein-like Patterns</span> to appear on her.
