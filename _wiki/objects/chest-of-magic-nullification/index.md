#Object 

A powerful artifact which suppresses all magic inside of it. It belonged to <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/ricio-tane/">Ricio Tane</a> and is now in the possession of <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/jean/">Jean</a> following <a class="wiki-link" href="/notes/session-10/">Session 10</a>. Before, it held a <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/strange-idol/">Strange Idol</a> which had "Cataclysmic effects". In the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/emporium-of-marvelous-rarities/">Emporium of Marvelous Rarities</a>, the party switched out the idol to make room for the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/crown-of-aglarion/">Crown of Aglarion</a>.
