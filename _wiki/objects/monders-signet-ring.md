A silver ring recovered from <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a>'s body after his demise. It contains a reference to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/monder-family-crypt/">Monder Family Crypt</a> as well as a hint to find the secret hallway and the sequence of doors to open to reach the vault: 2,4,2. The guardian of the vault accepted it as proof of permission.  
The ring is in possession of <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Nyla</span> following <a class="wiki-link" href="/notes/session-20-the-purge-pt-2/">Session 20 (The Purge pt 2)</a>.
