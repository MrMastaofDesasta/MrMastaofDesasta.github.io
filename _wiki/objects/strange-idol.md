Probably cursed, with effects described as "cataclysmic". <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/ricio-tane/">Ricio Tane</a> recounted his experience with it as feeling like his insides got pulled in all directions at once.  
  
Previously it was inside of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/chest-of-magic-nullification/">Chest of Magic Nullification</a>, but following <a class="wiki-link" href="/notes/session-14/">Session 14</a>, it was left behind in the antimagic vault underneath the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/emporium-of-marvelous-rarities/">Emporium of Marvelous Rarities</a>.
