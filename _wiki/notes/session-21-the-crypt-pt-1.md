- We regroup and take a brief moment to rest before heading out. Rozena is still not back yet. Jean tries to make Nyla lead the way to the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Monder Family Crypt</span>, but she is unable to focus properly and gets lost again and again until she asks Jean to lead instead.  
- Arriving at the crypt, we hear voices inside. There are <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Black Flowers</span> on the path leading to it. Leon sends his familiar, <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/chunk/">Chunk</a>, inside through a crack in the wall and spots two soldiers wearing red cloaks. Meanwhile, the vines around keep growing at a rapid pace.

A <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">purple mask</span> falls of their face and they turn into a different looking human. Nyla takes the mask.

Nyla can rearrange the skeleton to proper form, and offers a prayer with Brush, then a piece of candy, as well as her sincerity as a tribute.

- In the rest of the room, we find nothing of note, and nothing relating to the numbers 2,4,2. However, Nyla notices on Monder's ring that a piece of the wall in the north is disrupted on the ring's sigil. She then finds a button and opens a door, while the others were investigating a fireplace and pool of blood, presumably left by an intruder who was killed by the ghost.  
- However, the stair behind the secret door is trapped. Nyla and Jean disarm the trap. In the next room, we hear a voice underneath the floor and 3 doors. After the party opens all three doors, we get locked inside and the floor starts opening up. Jean jumps down and gets impaled on spikes, but can send the other person back up. Brush starts healing them. Urgo throws a rope down to get Jean up while Nyla freezes up and has a panic attack, as Jean's screams of pain threw her back to the day before, and the fear of losing someone else paralyzes her. She tells Jean not to do it again while crying. (quote)  
- Jean comforts Nyla and eventually we use the Master Key to open the right door and stop the trap. We however, cannot take a rest and have to continue to catch up to anyone who may be in front of us. Jean stops Brush from healing the stranger.  
- Urgo and Jean noticed traps on the next door and Jean and Leon disarmed them together, while Brush continued taking care of the injured person. Nyla crouched down on the stairs and tried to comfort herself by petting Amaterasu, but still cried silently. During the disarming, the ceiling started to lower, but snapped back when it was done. Beyond the door, a group of people noticed Urgo coming in, one of them being the leader of the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Loud and Lusty Brothel</span>, which Jean recognized.
