After the party reconvened in <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/jeans-apartment/">Jean&#x27;s Apartment</a>, following their individual tasks, they took a moment to rest before heading out again. Nyla quietly worked on assembling a crude prayer necklace from the holy symbols she recovered earlier, trying to keep her mind occupied and not worry about Rozena, who still had not come back. When leaving, Jean asked Nyla to lead the way as she usually did, but she was still not functioning properly and got lost, until Jean thankfully took over.  
The <a class="wiki-link" href="/other/monder-family-crypt/">Monder Family Crypt</a> was abandoned at first glance, vines growing around it and black flowers on the path leading towards it. However, voices could be heard from inside it as the party snuck closer. To get a better look, Leon summoned <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/chunk/">Chunk</a> and sent him inside through a crack in the corner to survey the room. Inside were two guards wearing red cloaks, but they seemed to be lacking the typical "knightly rigidity". Believing them to be impostors, the party made plans to avoid them, but due to there only being a single entrance, had to settle on sneakily opening the door and casting Sleep. One of the guards fell victim to the spell, but the other resisted. As he called out for the party to stop in the name of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Queen</a>, Nyla shot him with her rose magic, overcome with a sudden anger flaring up inside her. Jean finished him off, and while the party entered, Nyla ran up to him to try and stabilize him. While she was treating him, a <a class="wiki-link" href="/other/doppelganger-mask/">purple mask</a> fell of his face, which Nyla pocketed for later. He reverted to his true form, which was that of a human, but looking differently from the guard, more or less confirming the involvement of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/golden-masks/">Golden Masks</a>. Nyla and Brush finally finished treating him and joined the others, but before she left, Nyla whispered a guilt-ridden apology to him.  
Deeper inside the crypt, the party encountered the ghost of someone who seemed like a <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Monder Family</span> ancestor, floating inside a room where a sarcophagus had been opened and the skeleton disturbed. The ghost seemed agitated and becried the defilement of the crypt, demanding that that which had been wronged be righted.

While Nyla started offering a prayer to the Inevitable for the ones buried here, Jean and Urgo tried closing the sarcophagus again, but it didn't seem like it would be enough, so Jean called Nyla over. With her experience, she was able to rearrange the skeleton into a proper burial form, which seemed to please the ghost. Brush realized that a specific dwarven prayer might be needed, and he and Nyla were able to complete it. Brush offered a coin as tribute, while Nyla offered a piece of candy, due to not having anything else.

The rest of the room contained more burial places of members of the Monder family, but nothing relating to the sequence found on the ring, **2,4,2**. While the others went to investigate a spot of blood in the corner, where the ghost from earlier presumably attacked an intruder, they find a fire burning, and a common looking armor, Nyla investigated the ring again for clues. There was a sigil on it in the same shape of the room, but at one spot, the line representing the wall was disrupted. She went to that spot and found a button which opened a secret door to a hidden staircase.  
As the others joined her again, the party noticed a trap on the sides of the stair, which would make spears spring forth. As evidenced by the pool of blood and the footprints leading deeper inside, this already happened. Together, Jean and Nyla disarmed the trap after some trouble, and the party could head further inside.  
The next room they found themselves in had 3 possible exit doors, and muffled cries for help could be heard from underneath the floor. Jean made themselves, Urgo, and Leon open all doors simultaneously, which activated a trap, locking them inside and slowly opening the floor. Trying to help the person below, Jean jumped into the crack, but got impaled on the spikes below. However, they were able to float the other <a class="wiki-link" href="/other/stranger-from-the-crypt/">person</a> upwards, where Brush and Leon could grab them and start treating them. Meanwhile Urgo threw a rope down to help Jean up again. Unnoticed, Nyla got thrown back to the evening before after hearing Jean's screams and started hyperventilating, as the thought of losing another person paralyzed her. Jean noticed upon coming up and calmed her down.

Eventually, the party ended up in front of the correct door, and Jean used Nyla's <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/master-key/">Master Key</a> to open it and disarm the trap just in time. Though the party was all kinds of exhausted, they could not take a rest however, as others were presumably in front of them. Before continuing, Brush tried to heal the stranger, but got stopped by Jean and told to preserve his resources.  
However, before the party could continue, there was one more trapped door in the way, which Leon and Jean disarmed together, while Brush continued taking care of the injured person. Nyla sat down inside the staircase and pulled her legs close, trying to find comfort by playing with <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/amaterasu/">Amaterasu</a>, but couldn't suppress quiet tears which she hid from the others. Though the ceiling started lowering during the disarming, they eventually opened the door and stopped the trap. As Urgo stepped through the door, he was noticed by a group of people being led by a woman who Jean recognized as the <a class="wiki-link" href="/other/leader-of-the-loud-and-lusty-brothel/">leader of the Loud and Lusty Brothel</a>.
