As the night deepened and darkness fell, wrapping everything in a blanket of unknowing, the party was trying to find rest, and a measure of peace in <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/jeans-apartment/">Jean&#x27;s Apartment</a>. Brush laid awake, the experience of the day evoking vivid memories of an event from his past, him alone, in a tent, his legs burned, similar to how they were now. The feeling of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/ricio-tane/">Ricio</a>'s blood still lingered on his hands while he drifted off.  
Meanwhile Leon recalled the moment that Brush came stumbling out of the burning <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/lucky-leap-tavern/">Tavern</a>, carrying the body of Ricio, but in his dream, it almost seemed like the smoke was lingering, reaching out for him to pull him into the inferno. Eventually, Leon fell forward, into a pool of blood.  
Nyla on the other hand, did not dream of anything, as she only briefly awoke from her unconsciousness, long enough to feel <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Rozena</span>'s hand resting on her chest, before drifting off into sleep.  
Jean did not sleep, instead choosing to stand watch over the party. The night felt endless, stretching like thick honey smothering their thoughts, just like all days have felt recently. Their gaze wandered restlessly over their ruined apartment, where the layer of soot and ash covered their previous life, seeing <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Monder</a>'s hand gradually turn pale and eventually blue as the morning approached.  
  
# The Aftermath of Trauma  
The first to be up was <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-kiryn/">Kiryn</a>, suddenly standing in the door frame to the living room. Jean immediately went to him and tried to distract him from the bodies and the death around. They did not have the heart to tell him that they were all dead, so when Kiryn tried to touch Monder's hand, Jean picked him up and brought him out of the room, saying that Monder fought so bravely that he has to rest for a long time now.  
Nyla awoke slowly, staring up at the unfamiliar ceiling for a bit while the memories of the last evening came back to her, and a numbness settled over her mind. Eventually, she pushed herself up and scanned the room, finding only <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-finia/">Lady Finia</a> next to her at first glance. Panicking, she looked for <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Rozena</span>, before finding her floating behind her, and sighing with relief. Rozena filled her in on what happened after Nyla passed out, then said that she would be going to the other temples to look. Nyla is initially hesitant, since Rozena is bound to her as an anchor, but eventually agrees, weakly promising that she will be okay by herself, but also asking Rozena to be careful in return. After a few more minutes, Nyla got up and wrapped herself in her temple robes, praying to <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/the-inevitable/">The Inevitable</a> to ask them to be kind to the souls of her temple family. She tried to remember every person, but got distraught when she reached the people who she never learned the names of, or hadn't interacted much. The thought of them going unmourned, of no one alive noticing their passing drove silent tears into her eyes.  
At the same time in the living room, Brush awoke and got up, realizing with a look around all the corpses, that the last night was indeed not a dream. He went up to the body of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/gratus-ultio/">Gratus Ultio</a> and tried to revive him, but the spell failed as he had been dead for too long already. He went down the line, body after body, but none of them responded to his magic. Even <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/jeagan/">Jeagan</a> seemed to have quietly passed away in the night. Eventually, Leon woke up as well and asked Brush what he was doing. Brush lamented that nothing he tried was working and that they were truly dead. Leon comforted him, saying that maybe Brush just needed some rest, but Jean intervened, their nerves clearly raw from the night, and bluntly said that they most likely couldn't be brought back.  
However, Kiryn overheard the conversation and was distraught, not understanding what was happening. Jean cursed their carelessness under their breath and moved to comfort Kiryn, explaining death to him. They ended by saying that Kiryn should remember the dead not as they died, but as they lived. Kiryn then walked up to Monder and quietly said his goodbyes to him.

  <div class="callout-title">Jean to Kiryn</div>  
  <div class="callout-content">  
"They may be dead, and cannot come back, but that doesn't mean they are gone. As long as you remember them, they will forever live on in our hearts. So live and remember for as long as you can."  
  </div>  
</div>  
  
  
While supporting Kiryn during his goodbye, Jean noticed a silvery spark in Lord Monder's left hand, but didn't investigate further to give Kiryn his moment. Kiryn then said goodbye to Jeagan as well, when Nyla finished her prayers, feeling resolve for just a second before the sadness settled back over her. She joined the others in the living room, just as Jean was carrying Kiryn back into the bedroom where they settled him on a chair and gave him one of their instruments to play with. Meanwhile Nyla silently went up to the bodies and started performing last rites and prayers for them as well. Her body moved almost on its own, the prayer seeming almost reflexive, rather than an active decision.  
When Jean returned, they wanted to discuss the events and the attack on the tavern, especially the possibility of a mole among the original group. Nyla stopped her prayers to turn around and revealed, her voice cracking and whispered, that <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Aphina</a>'s forces didn't only attack the tavern, but also murdered all the people in the temples. Before she started to cry again, she managed to describe the scene of their bodies hanging from the bridges and the ceiling of the temple she visited. The others were shocked at the news and went to comfort Nyla, eventually calming her down a little unsure if that would be what her temple family would have wanted. Jean's rallying cry to take revenge on Aphina resonated with Nyla and she agreed to go on, although she was a little unsure if that would be what her temple family would have wanted. In the end, the whole party decided to stick together despite the setback and to remain in <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/aglarion/">Aglarion</a>, seeing this through to the end even with the very real possibility of all of them dying. Jean made Brush and Nyla promise though to prioritize their own lives and not sacrifice themselves needlessly. Especially directed at Nyla though.  
After the conversation, Nyla continued praying, while Jean investigated the silver object in Monder's hand. It was a signet ring bearing the same initials as <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Lord Monder&#x27;s Key</span>, and with the engraved word "Crypt", followed by the numbers **2,4,2**. Meanwhile Nyla found that one of the guards body had translucent skin with dark veins, and its limbs were longer than usual. She didn't know what that meant though, so Jean came over to investigate as well. They identified the body as a <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Doppelganger</span> and found a golden coin in its pocket with a mask symbol, the sign of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/golden-masks/">Golden Masks</a>.  
  
# Lone Wolves  
Jean pocketed the coin without anyone noticing it, then got up and shared their findings. As the rest of the party was still wondering what this meant or how the doppelganger got there, they said that they may have a way of finding out and turned to leave. Brush and Leon tried to stop them, but Jean asked them to trust them and left, getting struck by <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/hex-lightning/">Hex Lightning</a> on the way out.  
Nyla had turned away from the discussion and continued her work to distract herself from the mental image of her temple family hanging from the bridge, or the alternative - seeing herself digging her hands into Aphina's chest and slowly crushing her heart in bloody vengeance. It was going well, however when she was kneeling next to Jeagan, holding her hand and arranging her body while offering a prayer to <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/the-inevitable/">The Inevitable</a>, Nyla heard a voice from within herself, then experienced a vision of Jeagan's last moments.

> *Jeagan clung to the ladder leading out of the tavern, after maneuvering the pets outside of it, placing them next to a saddled horse in the hopes that the party would find them. Underneath her, in the room which the ladder connected to, she could hear arguing. Lord Monder, accusing some of his soldiers of betraying him. Then the fighting broke out, the air filling with the clanging sounds of metal hitting metal and the squelching of metal digging into flesh. Amidst the battle, one exclamation clearly reached Jeagan's ears. "Your thoughts betray you Lord Monder! We know about the journal you keep in the crypts!" She climbed further up, but felt her strength waning from the exertions of the evening. The tavern had simply grown too big of a hideout, too many people in one place. The queen's infernal soldiers had come in search of defectors and now traitors on top of that. Suddenly a columb of fire roared underneath her, consuming a figure with translucent, veiny skin who had reached inside after her. Then the flames reached her too, scalding her fingers and making her lose her grip. As Jeagan fell down the shaft, her last thoughts were dedicated to the fat cat in her arms. Then she hit the ground and a sharp pain stung in her spine, a jolt of fear streaking through her mind, before everything went dark.*  
  
The vision revealed that there were most likely multiple doppelgangers, however since the party only found the body of one, at least one more must have escaped with the information. The attack on the tavern also didn't seem to be deliberately targeting the rebellion, and was likely just because the queen was looking for any groups of people hiding. And finally, the doppelgangers didn't seem to have come looking for the journal specifically, instead having learned of it from reading Monder's mind, though not completely.  
Having experienced Jeagan's death firsthand, Nyla froze up and a tear ran down her cheek, which was noticed by Brush. When asked what happened, she told the others, who came to the conclusion that this was not something to ignore, as the enemies seemed to be after it. Brush then joined Nyla to finish the rites and prayers sooner, copying similar rites he saw at some point.  
When they finished, Nyla declared that she wanted to retrieve the prayer implements that were scattered by the bridge, as Jean had suggested earlier, wanting to retain at least this much from the people she lost, as it was impossible to properly bury the bodies. Brush tried to stop her from leaving and offered to go with her to keep her safe. While Nyla appreciated the gesture, she chose to leave by herself anyway, since she would be safer by herself, given her experience with remaining undetected. Brush relented eventually, but not before drawing a protective symbol of a lighthouse on her arm. Seeing the familiar building made Nyla tear up again and she hugged Brush, thanking him and promising to return within the hour. She started putting on her robes, but stopped, remembering that it wasn't smart to go out with a religious symbol, and instead folded it up to lay it with the pile of her belongings which she took out of her bag. Then she turned invisible to leave, leaving behind only the small pile of her belongings, her entire life packed up inside a bag.  
  
## Jean POV  
Jean, after being grazed by lightning as they were jumping out the window, was flying over <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/onadbyr/">Onadbyr</a>, intending to visit <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/tyx/">Tyx</a> and ask about the involvement of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/golden-masks/">Golden Masks</a>. While on the way, they remembered the patch of skin they cut off the dead cultist in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/monster-menagerie/">Monster Menagerie</a> with the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Strange Organization&#x27;s Tattoo</span>, and realized its similarity to [TODO: Ask and enter].  
Finally, they approached the familiar house, noticing that the normally well maintained garden was overgrowing with vines and flowers, the ones grown by the <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/cursed-rain/">Cursed Rain</a>. Even the front door was already starting to rot. Jean changed before knocking, however it took unusually long for a reaction to appear inside. Even then, Tyx didn't open until Jean announced themselves. She greeted them suspiciously, and insisted on a confirmation that they weren't followed. Eventually she let them in and quickly closed the door behind them. Despite her status as an informant, she seemed stressed and ignorant of most of the recent happenings. Before Jean could ask, she told them that she was preparing to leave <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/aglarion/">Aglarion</a> with her kids and escape to a different place, citing that "The Masks" have a presence everywhere, so she should be able to find work regardless of the place. With that, it was strongly implied that both Jean and Tyx were part of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/golden-masks/">Golden Masks</a>.  
When Jean told her about the attack on the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/lucky-leap-tavern/">Lucky Leap Tavern</a>, she didn't seem to know what they were talking about, and she asked what they had to do with it. Jean let out a sigh, but when they told Tyx that they had to come clean about something, she immediately asked if this was about "Jean" (Implying that Jean was not the identity they used to interact with the organization). Jean was surprised that she knew, but agreed that it was probably natural that she had sniffed it out already.  
Jean then pulled out the gold coin they found on the doppelganger earlier and, after explaining more about the party and their plan to stop <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/the-curse/">The Curse</a> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Aphina</a>, asks if they were in over their heads and what the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/golden-masks/">Golden Masks</a> had to do with it.  
Tyx listened to the story and stated that "They" (The Masks) work alone, though [Clarification Question asked to Forest]  
Tyx could only confirm that <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Aphina</a> was hunting all soldiers who refused to join her and holy people, and that the attack on the tavern was most likely because just too many people were gathered there and drew attention.  
However, Tyx was also clueless about the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">tattoo</span> on the skin patch when Jean asked her about it.  
At the end, Tyx offered to take <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-finia/">Lady Finia</a> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-kiryn/">Kiryn</a> with her when she would leave the country, as long as they weren't hunted specifically, as that would be too much risk for her. She also said that if she was in a dangerous situation and had to make a decision, she would prioritize herself and her kids. Jean would only have to get them to her in the night, or early in the morning. She would bring them at least to a safe point at the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Lighthouse</span>, and further if it seems safe. Jean thanks her and promises that they will not bring any problems with them.  
As they were parting, Tyx warns Jean never to lie to her again or else, and implies that she would appreciate payment for the smuggling.

## Nyla POV  
Having turned invisible, Nyla was sneaking out of the building, when the air crackled around her, and a <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/hex-lightning/">Hex Lightning</a> descended on her, as had happened so often to her already. For a moment it looked like she was going to let it hit herself, before the promises to live and come back unharmed sprung into her mind, and she sidestepped it at the last moment, though she got grazed by it.  
Back at the bridge, Nyla kept her eyes downcast and avoided looking directly at it, instead focusing on the cartloads of holy symbols scattered in big piles. Though the vast majority of them were crushed and broken, some still remained somewhat intact. As Nyla got to work to sort through all of them, a gradual sense of peace settled on her, making every new discovery feel just like a quiet cut into her soul, rather than a new blow to her sanity.  
As her exposed fingers got cold and clammy from the rain, <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/amaterasu/">Amaterasu</a> came out from underneath her coat and tried to warm them, but Nyla pushed it back underneath to protect it. She noticed, however, that by now its entire body was skeletal. Deciding to look into this later, she continued until she had filled her bag, and hiding other pieces in barrels around the area. A part of her was relieved since she didn't find <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/mia/">Mia</a>'s belongings.  
A bit more confident, Nyla turned to look at the bridge for the first time and stood up, clasping her hands as she made a silent vow to the deceased to carry their expectations forward and to become the future they lost. However, as she did, Amaterasu suddenly went limp underneath her cloak. Panicking, she slumped down and made a tent of her coat to look inside, seeing its lifeless body on her lap. Although her rational mind was afraid that she'd lost another one, a part of her felt that this was not wrong. Indeed, soon its body began to heat up and eventually disintegrate into ash, burning hot on her hands. She hurriedly ran to a roof to shelter the ash from the rain more safely, where it formed into a newborn Solar Kitten. Nyla hugged it and thanked it for not having died too. Still confused but glad, she returned to Jean's apartment.  
  
# Plans  
When Jean returner to the apartment ahead of Nyla, they found Brush performing magic for Evra. Brush explained to them that Nyla stepped out, which worries them but they agreed that Nyla is probably the most capable to avoid and escape trouble. Glad to have the opportunity, they gathered the others and revealed that the tavern was most likely attacked due to the large number of defectors and clerics hidden inside, many of which were saved by Nyla. They swore the others to secrecy, in an effort not to burden Nyla anymore. Evra did not understand and asked why to keep it a secret, so Jean explained.  
  
<div class="callout callout-quote">  
  <div class="callout-title">Jean to Evra</div>  
  <div class="callout-content">  
"Sometimes doing something good leads to a bad outcome. It doesn't mean that the act itself was a mistake, but it can still make the person feel guilty."  
  </div>

They exchanged information and Jean learned about Nyla's vision just before she finally returned. She recounted the vision again, closing quietly that Jeagan bravely rescued all the party pets, but that her end was painful and filled with fear, unlike the time she herself died. In return, Jean shared the story of how they met Jeagan back then, and how she was fully prepared to fight off the entire party with nothing more than a wooden spoon. Hearing the story made Nyla cry again, due to the familiarity she now felt with the woman.  
The party made the plan for the day, first to go to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/other/monder-family-crypt/">Monder Family Crypt</a> and look for <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Lord Monder&#x27;s Journal</span> before the doppelgangers or whoever they serve could find it. On the way back, they were planning to search through the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/lucky-leap-tavern/">Lucky Leap Tavern</a> again in the hopes of finding any useful magic items that <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/ricio-tane/">Ricio</a> left behind.  
While the others got into an argument about the incident with the Kraken, Nyla stood up and stored the prayer implements she retrieved and repacked her bag, though leaving something this valuable to her behind made her feel uneasy. She also started picking out symbols to incorporate into her own necklace which she pulled out of her pile of belongings.  
Rozena still wasn't back, which was starting to worry her.
