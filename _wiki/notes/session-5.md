The party was continuing the games at the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/kings-games/">King&#x27;s Games</a>, where Urgo took part in the Merfolk Lumberjacks and won handily due to his ability to breathe underwater. The crowd was not too pleased by this landslide win, but he managed to not be disqualified. Jean spotted a <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Merfolk Person from the Kings Games</span> walking away.  
  
After the game, Jean split from the party and went of into the city, while the rest took another way. They came across a group of people gathering under a bridge, examining a mysterious writing on one of the support beams. Leon climbed up to read it.  
<div class="callout callout-quote">  
  <div class="callout-title">Bridge Hint</div>  
  <div class="callout-content">  
"to find your next clue, look under the venerable, tread through his bile most unbearable"  
  </div>  
</div>  
  
  
Shrugging it off for now, the party continued on their way to the next game, where they came across a first aid camp set up. Multiple clerics of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/church-of-the-redeemer/">Church of the Redeemer</a> were treating people being pulled out of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/sewers-under-onadbyr/">Sewers under Onadbyr</a>, many unconscious. A temple servant, <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Nyla</span> spotted them and approached them to see if they needed help. Upon hearing about the message, they decided to go down into the sewers together, Nyla quickly checking with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/mia/">Mia</a> (a cleric of the Redeemer) to see if it was okay. In the sewers, the party, especially Leon, was quickly overwhelmed by the stench, which made continuing difficult. Eventually, they found a message scribbled on the walls of a room there in large letters.  
<div class="callout callout-quote">  
  <div class="callout-title">Sewer Hint</div>  
  <div class="callout-content">  
"to find your treasure, you need to do less than you expect, visit the green giant, and to the bugs you must pay your respect"  
  </div>  
</div>  
  
  
Nyla's patron told her that it most likely referred to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/napping-giant/">Napping Giant</a>, a statue in a park in the upper class district. She also found traces underneath the letters which could evidence that the person who wrote the message came here in high heeled shoes. The writing style was almost calligraphic, making it likely that the author of the hints was a noblewoman. (<a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Noblewoman behind the treasure hunt</a>). Leon almost passed out just before leaving the sewers and contracted sewer plague, but Brush managed to heal him. Nyla (and her patron) led the party towards the Sleeping Giant. While they successfully snuck past the guards who were locking the park down, they ran into a swarm of bugs that attacked them. While the others were fighting, Nyla managed to get a look at the message left behind in the stone.  
<div class="callout callout-quote">  
  <div class="callout-title">Sleeping Giant Hint</div>  
  <div class="callout-content">  
"You proved you are persistent, the treasure is no longer mine, it awaits you high in the crown, at the feet of the nine"  
  </div>  
</div>  
  
  
Nyla's patron guessed that this refers to a tree in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/nine-arches/">Nine Arches</a>. Meanwhile, Jean had gone of on their own little adventure, which the rest was not privy to, but it also led them back to the party at this point. Together, they destroyed the bugs, but the reunion alerted the guards. Jean and Nyla managed to sneak away, but when they turned around they found Leon, Urgo, and Brush still standing around the statue, with guards closing in on their location.
