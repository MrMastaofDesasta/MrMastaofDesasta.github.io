The party went to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/nine-arches/">Nine Arches</a> and found a potion shelf balancing high up in a tree. Brush flew up using one of his abilities to grab it, but the others, barring Nyla, from below spotted a cut on the branch which was made to break easily. Despite warnings, Brush grabbed the shelf and sent the branch falling. The party quickly got the bystanders to get out of the way in time. However, it turned out the potions were some nasty smelling concoctions left behind by <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a> as a prank. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-rozena-von-vyne/">Rozena</a> commented on her disdain for her. As Aphina drove away, Urgo threw one of the concoctions against her cart, prompting everyone to flee. The party then continued the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/kings-games/">King&#x27;s Games</a>.

##### The Greasy Pole
The next game was the Greasy Pole, a game where the participants had to climb a pole covered in oily grease. Before the game, Jean went off to look for the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Merfolk Person from the Kings Games</span> that they saw during the Diving game, while Brush painted some faces and Nyla went to get snacks. During the game, Leon won handily while Nyla fell flat on her face but tried again without success. 

##### The Wild Hunt
Afterwards they went to the Wild Hunt, a game hosted by <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/hularis-aspora/">Hularis Aspora</a>, the head cleric of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/church-of-the-eternal-mother/">Church of the Eternal Mother</a>, at the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/onadbyr-temple-of-the-eternal-mother/">Onadbyr Temple of the Eternal Mother</a>, which Nyla won just barely. While the others left for the next game, Nyla took some time to place an offering and prayer for the Eternal Mother, and Brush waited for her.

##### The Leap of Death
At the location of the Leap of Death, Nyla and Jean spot a royal looking person taking part in the challenge. Nyla refused to take part and instead went to help out with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/mia/">Mia</a> and the other clerics of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/church-of-the-redeemer/">Church of the Redeemer</a> to treat the injured after their fall. During the game, Brush got hurt during his jump and the blood attracted a bunch of sharks while Nyla was helping him get out. Jean managed to send the closest one to sleep, so everyone was able to get out easily, and Nyla saved another person.

At the end, an announcement was made for the final event, the Battle of Champions, to begin in 3 hours.
