#Notes 

The party gathered in the evening for the heist into the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/emporium-of-marvelous-rarities/">Emporium of Marvelous Rarities</a>. As planned, they split into two groups: Jean and Brush taking one route, and Nyla and Leon taking another, with Urgo waiting outside.
Nyla opened the front door with the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/master-key/">Master Key</a>, and Jean and Brush slipped inside to flip the first switch. Meanwhile, Nyla and Leon climbed up to a balcony to find their way in, where they were met with three spells that needed to be dispelled. Behind a hidden door Nyla found, they discovered <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/evra/">Evra</a>, a flesh construct of some kind, apparently built within the Emporium itself by <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/master-bertio-ranberd/">Master Bertio Ranberd</a>. She was deeply curious about magic and immediately latched onto Leon. She could also, notably, see through invisibility. 

<div class="callout callout-quote">
  <div class="callout-title">Evra</div>
  <div class="callout-content">
"I was made here" 
"You are interesting. You can do magic. I will come with you" 
"I am very well behaved"
  </div>

Nyla flipped the switch, and she and Leon promised Evra they would return before pressing on.
Jean and Brush moved through two corridors filled with a disorienting mist before reaching a library. They were forced to briefly retreat when a woman entered through another door, but managed to evade her. Brush copied a spell from the collection while Jean browsed.
Nyla and Leon crossed a hallway but quickly lost their bearings in the confusion — only <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-rozena-von-vyne/">Lady Rozena von Vyne</a> kept them oriented toward the next room. There, Nyla found a painting of <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/house-gorso/">House Gorso</a>'s clocktower but no switch. While Leon took over the search and found it, Nyla helped herself to a self-refilling jug she came across. Leon took two spellbooks. Nyla cut a painting of <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/gorso-village/">Gorso Village</a> — where <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/the-little-home/">The Little Home</a> orphanage once stood — from its frame and tucked it away.
Jean and Brush located the final switch after Jean received another contact through <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/jeans-ring-of-mindshielding/">Jean&#x27;s Ring of Mindshielding</a>.
With all three switches flipped, the party retrieved <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/evra/">Evra</a> and lowered her down a rope to Urgo before regrouping with Jean and Brush. Together they descended into the basement, put a sentinel to sleep, deactivated an animated armor trap, and passed through the first vault into the second.

#### The Second Vault
The second vault held a portal, supposedly leading to the actual vault beyond. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-rozena-von-vyne/">Lady Rozena von Vyne</a> chose to stay behind, uncertain whether the antimagic on the other side would affect her. The party went through alone.
Inside, they found a large green glowing crystal (Possibly the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Crystal of the Immortals</span>???) — and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/master-bertio-ranberd/">Master Bertio Ranberd</a>, standing and waiting. An immense mental pain assailed the party as he made them an offer.

"Thy m’rtal souls shall anon beest dissolv’d by the residue of the seraph’s might. Leaveth anon and nev’r returneth. Thee couldst toucheth the crystal and taketh pow'r far beyond thy m'rtal comprehension"

Nyla, intrigued by the promise of power, stepped forward. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/master-bertio-ranberd/">Master Bertio Ranberd</a> revealed himself to be a <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/couatl/">Couatl</a> and spoke to her of her potential. But the power he offered was rooted in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/dark-star/">Dark Star</a>, and Nyla, afraid of what <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-rozena-von-vyne/">Lady Rozena von Vyne</a> would think of her for taking it, refused. She turned away, and the party fled.

Meanwhile Jean had quietly emptied the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/chest-of-magic-nullification/">Chest of Magic Nullification</a>.
