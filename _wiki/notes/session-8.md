To start the session, Leon fought a battle in the Battle of Champions (<a class="wiki-link" href="/events/kings-games/">King&#x27;s Games</a>) in <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/the-dog-pit/">The Dog Pit</a>, which he handily won thanks to his magic. Being called up right again for the second round, he also defeats Jean. As the only one in the party left, Nyla is called up to fight against <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/gratus-ultio/">Gratus Ultio</a>, a paladin of <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/the-shining-light-and-the-darkness-within/">The Shining Light and The Darkness Within</a>. With the help of Rozena, she defeats him with relative ease, but Rozena's gesture of respect is taken up quite well. Brush goes to talk to him to learn more about the Shining Light specifically. During the show, Jean meets a woman by the name of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/rifa/">Rifa</a> and her child, to whom he immediately strikes up a conversation.
### Regicide
In the middle of these matches, a knight in dark armor steps up and upon winning their battle, issues a direct challenge to <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-waldrann-azennar/">King Waldrann Azennar</a>. He accepts, confidently, and steps into the arena. The exchange lasts a short while, but in the end, the dark knight manages to get a few decisive hits in, bringing the battle to a close with the king's surrender. This catches everyone by surprise, considering the king's perfect winning streak. 
However, when King Waldrann invites the dark knight to reveal their face and claim their price for winning this bout, a stronger shock runs through the crowd. The dark knight reveals the face of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/former-queen-alyssa-azennar/">Former Queen Alyssa Azennar</a>, and claims Waldrann a kinslayer. 

<div class="callout callout-quote">
  <div class="callout-title">Former Queen Alyssa to King Waldrann</div>
  <div class="callout-content">
"Waldrann, you are a kinslayer and traitor to your house! Not even death can avenge your deeds. I claim your soul as punishment. It is in Hell where we shall meet again!"
  </div>
</div>


Jean recognizes her immediately, as does Rozena who is shocked and frozen in place by the revelation. In the silence, Alyssa strikes again, beheading King Waldrann. Before anyone can react, she revealsa pair of dark <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Large Wings</span>, which seem devilish in nature, before a magical circle forms behind her and she disappears. (<a class="wiki-link" href="/events/murder-of-king-waldrann/">Murder of King Waldrann</a>)

For a few seconds, the crowd seems to hold their breath, as clerics rush out to attempt a resurrection. Jean starts ushering the party, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/rifa/">Rifa</a> and her child, as well as <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-finia/">Lady Finia</a> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-kiryn/">Lord Kiryn</a> together to start heading out. Finally, when the resurrections fails, and the clerics have to announce the death of King Waldrann, the crowd finally breaks out in a panic and rushes out. Jean maneuvers the party and companions out safely.

### Aftermath
As they are about to leave the area, the party is approached by a guard who asks <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-finia/">Lady Finia</a> to accompany them to <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a>. She agrees to come, together with Urgo who comes with to keep them safe. Nyla, being surprised by Rozena's strong reaction, also decides to come along, though she follows invisibly, in order to maybe glean more information. Being without anything to do, the rest continue to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/partys-house/">Party&#x27;s House</a>. On the streets, the people seem distraught, grieving for the king as word spreads, and the atmosphere is grim. Some are panicking and wailing.

Back in the arena, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a> greets <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-finia/">Lady Finia</a> and thanks Urgo, asking him as a representative of the party to attend <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/king-waldranns-funeral-procession/">King Waldrann&#x27;s Funeral Procession</a> the next morning. (Apparently this is very normal to have so soon and not a cause for suspicion, source DM). As they shake hands, it seems he wants to say something more, but only Nyla picks up on that, and she, being invisible, has no way to tell Urgo. Immediately after the funeral, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a> is set to be crowned, which Rozena comments on with concern. While Urgo heads back, Nyla stays to listen in more, and quickly grabs a <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/vial-of-king-waldranns-blood/">Vial of King Waldrann&#x27;s Blood</a> from the scene. As she listens in on the guards talking, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a> stops them, saying it is "just rumors" and tells them to talk about it later tonight. He seems genuinely distraught as well, clasping his hands, and starts to talk about planning the funeral.
Nyla follows him and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-finia/">Lady Finia</a> for a bit more but does not learn anything more, so she eventually starts heading back and reunites with <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/amaterasu/">Amaterasu</a>. While heading back to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/partys-house/">Party&#x27;s House</a>, the location of which she received from Jean via Sending, she asks Rozena about the former queen.

### Back at Home
Back in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/partys-house/">Party&#x27;s House</a>, Leon, Brush, and Jean are thinking about the magic they saw behind <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/former-queen-alyssa-azennar/">Former Queen Alyssa Azennar</a> before she disappeared. They draw a comparison to the one they saw at <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/the-old-grinder/">The Old Grinder</a> and manage to determine that it seems similar in nature, but antithetical. Specifically, like one brought something, while the other one took something. From that, they conclude that Alyssa was likely summoned by someone and now that her purpose was fulfilled, the magic that kept her here was dismissed. 

<div class="callout callout-quote">
  <div class="callout-title">Jean about King Waldrunn</div>
  <div class="callout-content">
The king and this kingdom gave us everything! We can only live in luxury and peace because of everything he did for us.
  </div>
</div>


Jean tries to rile the rest of the party towards avenging the king because they owe him that much. When Nyla arrives, she has a loud disagreement with him, as she is of the opinion that the King and country have never done anything for her, and that even a bad ruler ultimately makes no difference to those at the true bottom of society. So she feels no particular reason to avenge him or seek justice, as to her he was just another guy at the top. 
After both storm off, steaming from the disagreement, Brush takes Nyla to an empty room where she can stay the night and asks for advice. He does not know what to do and wonders if Nyla truly believes what she said. She reiterates her point, albeit calmer.

<div class="callout callout-quote">
  <div class="callout-title">Nyla about Rulers and Power</div>
  <div class="callout-content">
A bad ruler is one that takes away power from others and keeps it themselves. But for those at the true bottom of society, the ones that don't have any power, it makes no difference. You cannot take from the ones that had nothing to start with. That's why I don't believe a bad ruler is going to make a true difference, not in the ways that matter.
  </div>
</div>


As Brush asks her what to do now, she in turn asks him to first think about what it is he wants to achieve. He answers that he wants to fix the world, to bring it back to the happy mood it was just before the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/murder-of-king-waldrann/">Murder of King Waldrann</a>. Nyla thinks for a second, then tells him to start by learning whatever he can about the situation, and then take the next step towards action once he can see clearly.
As the evening passes, each partymember does their own thing. Brush draws in his room, replicating things from his <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/nightmares/">Nightmares</a>. No matter what he draws, they always come out in a <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Set of Three</span>. Urgo cries himself to sleep. Jean gets a bottle of wine and goes to Leon's room to talk about the day and get drunk. They learn about <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/erinyes/">Erinyes</a>, a type of vengeful devil, whose description seems to fit <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/former-queen-alyssa-azennar/">Former Queen Alyssa Azennar</a>. They determine that the king probably did "something" to provoke such a reaction, as a soul does not simply turn into a devil for no reason. Nyla performed a ritual to make <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/nylas-pact-weapon/">Nyla&#x27;s Pact Weapon</a> absorb the power of the magical silver shortsword she won in the Battle of Champions. Afterwards, she asks Rozena how she feels about all this, considering her relationship with the queen. However, Rozena as usual tries to turn the therapy back on Nyla. She only says that she was shocked to see Alyssa come back so changed, especially knowing her as a softer kind of person. It made her rethink her opinion of the king. Even though she didn't initially like him much, she came to regard him with neutrality. But now she believes he may have actually done bad things. She muses about the time when the current queen was kidnapped into the Feywild and the subsequent events that led to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/death-of-prince-krasnar/">Death of Prince Krasnar</a>. The king and son (Krasnar) went to retrieve her. On the way back, the son was supposedly taken by quicksand, although there were rumors.
