As Urgo stepped through the door after it was unlocked by Jean and Leon, he was greeted by a group of people, centered around <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/leader-of-the-loud-and-lusty-brothel/">leader of the Loud and Lusty Brothel</a>, a woman wearing expensive headgear and clothes who was the owner of the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Loud and Lusty Brothel</span>. While Urgo's calling her a "bug woman" and rambling threatened to escalate the situation, Jean could intervene due to being acquainted with her, and deescalate. The group was there only to find "the riches" and just seemed to have taken the opportune moment to break into the crypt, rather than being affiliated with the <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Queen</a> or the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/golden-masks/">Golden Masks</a>. As Jean was dealing with the leader, they were reminded of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/ricio-tane/">Ricio</a> from the way she gestured and bore herself.  
As the negotiations relaxed and both groups agreed to share any findings 50/50, the atmosphere got lighter as introductions were made. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/leader-of-the-loud-and-lusty-brothel/">leader of the Loud and Lusty Brothel</a> offered a business card to Urgo and Nyla. Urgo refused due to his wife, but Nyla blushed upon realizing what she held and quickly put the card away, then wandered away while being teased by Jean. The room also contained 4 possible exit doors, and small piles of sand on the floor implied that triggering a trap would fill the entire room with it. After a brief examination, during which Brush got distracted by Jean with the task to paint the room and Urgo kept a suspicious eye on the other people, Leon opened the correct door and found another corridor leading further in. The other group did not follow though, instead leaving the further exploration to the party.  
As Leon entered the corridor, he triggered a floortile trap and barely managed to jump back. Despite his attempts to test the other tiles carefully using his spiderclimb, Jean triggered the next one, making it clear that they react to weight. Nyla could jump across the gap and lay a footrest for the others using Urgo's immovable rod. However, Brush still fell in, finding a thick, pungent, green liquid below. Eventually, by Jean casting Levitate on Urgo, the party was able to clear the hallway and found four more doors at the end.  
Nyla opened the farthest one, since it did not seem trapped, but encountered a strange snake-like creature behind it which used some magic to make her suspicious of the rest of the party. When she returned, she seemed standoffish and kept her distance, eyeing the party with her hand on the hilt of her sword. Leon discovered that only one of the doors was locked and the party managed to break the lock. Behind the door, Leon encountered the same creature, however now it talked, asking for proof that the party was allowed inside the crypt and only those of Monder mas pass.

Leon asked Nyla for the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/monders-signet-ring/">signet ring</a> which she did not hand over as she was still suspicious of them, instead showing it herself. But upon seeing the creature, the magic fell off of her and she came back to her senses. The guardian seemed satisfied with that and let the party pass, but asked them to leave at least one thing behind so it would have something to guard.  
Inside the vault, Nyla quickly searched for the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/lord-monders-journal/">journal</a> but took a bit to find it while the rest skimmed through the other items. The party discussed how to proceed from here, as no one felt comfortable with the idea to have the other group, who seem just like opportunistic treasure hunters, raid the vault of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a> who was a dear friend to most of the party. Jean stormed off upon seeing that the rest seemed very happy to indulge their greed and raid the vault themselves.  
Brush took a few healing potions, while Leon found some scrolls to take with him. Nyla took an <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/ioun-stone-of-protection/">Ioun Stone of Protection</a> with the insignia of a rose that resonated with her, but left a gold coin in return. The vault contained many other things, including several paintings of <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Monder Ancestor</span>, the ancestor whose ghost the party encountered earlier. As the party left, Nyla and Brush offered a prayer to thank and apologize for taking things from the vault. However, before leaving finally, Nyla turned back to fill a pouch with 100 gold coins, then asked the guardian to please lock the vault again and that whoever came after them would not belong to the party.  
Back in the last room, a stand off between the party and the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">other group</span> had begun. They demanded to see their half of the spoils and realizing that Leon lied to them about there not being anything were preparing to battle. Nyla intervened by approaching them peacefully and placed the pouch on the ground. Being honest, she explained that they left most things behind in the vault and that the party closed the vault again and that they would not help rob their own friend's family crypt. As a peace offering, she asked the other group to just take this gold and go, as it would be dangerous to continue. When <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/leader-of-the-loud-and-lusty-brothel/">leader of the Loud and Lusty Brothel</a> got angry at her for breaking the deal, Nyla responded that the deal was for the party to help, not that they would do all the work alone. Though dissatisfied the others accepted that after Nyla explained the way to the vault, but held back how to convince the guardian. After the others left behind half of their spoils as well, the party was able to avoid a battle. On the way out, Nyla checked on the impostor guard she treater earlier and left her waterskin behind for him.  
  
Meanwhile, Jean returned to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/lucky-leap-tavern/">Lucky Leap Tavern</a> ahead of the rest of the party to reminisce about the good times they had with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/ricio-tane/">Ricio</a> and the others here. They are sad that the others seem to have forgotten so quickly and moved on. Brush and Urgo seemed all too carefree messing around in the crypt, Leon just cared about his magic and the treasure, and Nyla having her own issues to deal with. Jean threw a coin to the counter to "pay" for the drinks they tooky then waited outside the tavern for the party to come back.  
Eventually the party reconvened after a conversation in the rain where Nyla expressed sadness that people seemed so willing to inflict pain on themselves and others just for greed. Inside the tavern, Jean and Leon indeed found a few items:  
> An unidentified Idol  
> A golden Tiara (500gp)  
> Circlet of Blasting  
> A sword inlaid with tiny diamonds (replica of the falcon blade) (2000gp)  
> 200gp  
> Various normal weapons and equipment  
> "Hell's Water" (Essentially a barrel of oil)  
> 3 potions of invisibility  
> 2 potions of greater healing  
> universal solvent  
> decorated handmirror  
>  
  
Nyla wandered off after being shocked by the destruction, as she had only caught a glimpse yesterday before she passed out. Her feet carried her to the sleeping quarters, where the reality of the situation hit her fully. Seeing the scattered and hastily left behind remains of the people who were now dead overwhelmed her and she set to clean up and organize the things. Halfway through however, she remembered the <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/baby-from-onadbyr/">toddler</a> the party picked up earlier, whose body had not been recovered. She rushed through the house to find them or their body, clearly panicking before being caught by Jean and Brush. Nyla explained her worries and the suspicion that maybe the toddler was what provoked the attack. Jean calmed her down somewhat and explained that this was unlikely. Eventually Nyla confessed to her feelings of guilt at being the only survivor of the clergy and many other places.  
Brush cast Calm Emotions on her to calm her down, which worked but made Nyla numb to all emotions after having so many of them raging inside of her all day. She went down to the cellar to perform last rites on the bodies left behind, tidy but also emotionally cold and robotically, lacking the emotional depth and sincerity she put into her prayers before. When the spell ended, she threw up with stress as her repressed emotions hit her again.
