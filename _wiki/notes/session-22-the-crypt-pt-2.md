As Urgo stepped through the door after it was unlocked by Jean and Leon, he was greeted by a group of people, centered around <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/leader-of-the-loud-and-lusty-brothel/">leader of the Loud and Lusty Brothel</a>, a woman wearing expensive headgear and clothes who was the owner of the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Loud and Lusty Brothel</span>. While Urgo's calling her a "bug woman" and rambling threatened to escalate the situation, Jean could intervene due to being acquainted with her, and deescalate. The group was there only to find "the riches" and just seemed to have taken the opportune moment to break into the crypt, rather than being affiliated with the <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Queen</a> ir the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/golden-masks/">Golden Masks</a>. As Jean was dealing with the leader, they were reminded of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/ricio-tane/">Ricio</a> from the way she gestured and bore herself.  
As the negotiations relaxed and both groups agreed to share any findings 50/50, the atmosphere got lighter as introductions were made. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/leader-of-the-loud-and-lusty-brothel/">leader of the Loud and Lusty Brothel</a> offered a business card to Urgo and Nyla. Urgo refused due to his wife, but Nyla blushed upon realizing what she held and quickly put the card away, then wandered away while being teased by Jean. The room also contained 4 possible exit doors, and small piles of sand on the floor implied that triggering a trap would fill the entire room with it.  
  
- Start in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/monder-family-crypt/">Monder Family Crypt</a> after escaping the room with the floor trap and opening the next door, where we are confronting a group of intruders.  
- Urgo rambles and almost escalates the situation to a fight but Jean can deescalate and strike a deal to work together to find the "riches". The leader's hand gesture reminds them of Ricio. She offers Urgo and Nyla a businesscard, which Urgo refuses and Nyla blushes before wandering somewhere else to investigate the room. There are little piles of sand, implying that a trap triggered would fill the room with sand. We investigate all the doors while Urgo keeps an eye on the other group and Brush gets distracted by Jean with the task to paint the room.  
- As Leon enters the corridor behind the correct door, he can barely jump back before a floorplate breaks away into a deep pit. Though he can check the rest of the tiles and does not detect another trap, Jean's weight activates another one, making it clear this trap is not as easy as it seems. Nyla can jump across and lay a footrest for Urgo and Brush to follow using Urgo's immovable rod. But Urgo falls into a thick green, foul smelling liquid. Urgo pulls him out and after a brief talk with the other group, where they refuse to go first, Brush cleans himself up. Jean casts Levitate on Brush and together they start following while Nyla and Leon scout further ahead and discover a hallway with four doors.  
- Nyla investigates and opens the furthest one. In a corridor she encounters a strange creature which magically makes her suspicious of the rest of the party. As the others do shenanigans, they discover a slight evocation magic on all the doors, but Leon's door is locked, so Nyla unlocks it after making Leon get out of the way. Upon unlocking it, she moves back and lets the others go in first, having her hand on her sword and surveying.  
- Leon opens the door and finds a hallway where the same creature is curled up, saying that only those of Monder may pass. Leon asks for the signet ring, but Nyla is too suspicious to hand it over so she instead goes to show it herself. Upon seeing the creature, her suspiciousness stops. The guardian agrees to let us in, but tells us to leave one thing behind to guard.  
- Inside Nyla quickly locates the journal before the party discusses how to continue. They feel dissatisfied with the idea of leaving all this stuff to the other group and Jean storms off, not wanting to rob the crypt of Monder who was our friend.  
- Brush takes a couple healing potions, Leon takes a couple scrolls, Nyla takes a Ioun Stone of Protection but leaves a gold coin in return. As the party returns, Nyla and Brush offer a prayer to both thank and apologize for taking things. Nyla then asks the guardian to lock the vault again.  
- As we return, the other group tries to get their half. While Leon lied to them, they are getting aggressive, when Nyla brings a pouch of a hundred gold coins she took from the vault as a peace offering. She tells the group that Monder was a friend and she's not going to help them rob his family crypt. The vault is closed and if they are truly wanting to, they can go further, but she asks them to instead be content with the gold. In the end, the part ends up giving them parts of their spoils and describes the way to the vault, but not how to get past the guardian.  
- Jean meanwhile goes to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/lucky-leap-tavern/">Lucky Leap Tavern</a> and reminisces about Ricio and Monder, lamenting that everyone seems to have forgotten them and goofed around in the crypt. He takes a drink and pays with a gold coin.  
- As the party reconvenes after a conversation while walking through the rain, Jean and Leon find a few items (which ones, ask for list). Nyla wanders off and enters the sleeping quarters where she gets once again overwhelmed by the reality of the situation upon seeing the hastily left remnants of life that has since been extinguished. But as she starts tidying up a bit, sorting through the belongings, she suddenly gets the thought of the toddler the party received just yesterday and rushes through the house to find him(?). Jean catches her to calm her down, where Nyla confesses to her feelings of guilt, until Brush casts Calm Emotions on her. Her mind being blank, she insists that she is fine and robotically performs last rites for the corpses left in the cellar. The scene afterwards feels tidy and clean, but cold. It lacks the depth of emotion which Nyla put into the rites this morning, and her usual prayers. At the end, the spell effect ends, and Nyla gets hit again by the full weight of her emotions, making her throw up with stress.
