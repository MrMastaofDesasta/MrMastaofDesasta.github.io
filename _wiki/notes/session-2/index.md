#Notes
During the night. <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/brush/">Brush</a> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/urgo/">Urgo</a> had <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/nightmares/">Nightmares</a>. Both struggled to wake up and when they did Urgo had a gold coin in his hand (in his dream he caught a gold fish). When they put the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/nightmare-coin/">Nightmare Coin</a> (there is nothing on either face of the coin) in water silver vein-like embossments appear. 

They headed down to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/royal-stud-farm/">Royal Stud Farm</a>. Which was pretty much rubble aside from major structures. They examined some of the dead bodies. One in particular caught their attention as it seemed to be '<span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">soulless</span>' . They heard a small meowing from the back and, after a lot of investigation, found <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/jeagan/">Jeagan</a> the royal chef and the cat <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/lord-tunalias-tibberton-rumlyn-the-fat/">Lord Tunalias Tibberton Rumlyn &#x27;The Fat&#x27;</a> . Jeagan was incredibly panicked and explained that <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/the-countess/">The Countess</a> (the royal groomer) had arrived with cloaked figures and taken the Lord Rumlyns (the father, grandfather, mother, and child) and to please save them. They had tried to warn Lord Monder about 'cult-like' activity happening at the abandoned windmill (<a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/the-old-grinder/">The Old Grinder</a>).

The party sent her back to the camp where she started to prepare them some food. And they headed to The Old Grinder. They managed to very cleverly sneak up and enter. They cleared the ground floor and headed downstairs. 

In the basement there was a giant red circle with glowing runes and half melted candles. In the center was the bodies of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-rumlyn/">Lord Rumlyn</a> (the dad) and his father.  

They also saw <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-kiryn/">Lord Kiryn</a> (the child) and his mother bound and gagged. Upon seeing the party, the countess set the cultists and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/eldon-lobo/">Eldon Lobo</a> (a devlish looking monk) to kill the boy and mom and the party. Saying "You will be disciplined for your intrusion, like <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/the-matrons/">The Matrons</a> disciplined the fool Lord Rumlyn for nisiness. Kill them. Kill them all". 

During the combat the mother was killed. But the party managed to knock the devil out and clear the rest of the basement.
