After finally vanquishing <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/old-gnarly/">Old Gnarly</a> in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/monster-menagerie/">Monster Menagerie</a>, the party relaxed for a moment. Even though Nyla went looking for the person she spotted earlier, they were nowhere to be found, not even traces remaining where they stood. Jean, Urgo, and Brush took care of the zookeper and placed him in the giftshop. The party noted that some <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Black Flowers</span> seemed to have started growing following Old Gnarly's death, but unable to find out more, they continued until they found a greenhouse in which several dead cultists laid, seemingly cut by a sharp weapon. Urgo recognized them as probably belonging to the same organization that kidnapped <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Urgo&#x27;s Wife</span>. All of them had a weird symbol tattooed on their necks.  
While the rest of the party was still investigating the greenhouse, Urgo crushed one of the cultists' skull, which caused an explosion of dark dust and energy, hazing everyone's thoughts for a second. The explosion woke up a shambling mound which attacked.  
After defeating that monster as well, Jean investigated the bodies further, noticing that the tattoo is similar to his own, and cut one off the body, which was noticed only by Nyla. (<span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Strange Organization&#x27;s Tattoo</span>)  
  
Before continuing, Nyla fell back to talk to Rozena, as she suspected the person watching her earlier (<span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Person watching during the Old Gnarly fight</span>) could have been a druid using pass without trace. Rozena agreed that it could have been a possibility but didn't commit to an answer.  
Next, the party came by a slightly damaged aviary, inside of which used to be some <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Peacockatrice</span>, a rare kind of creature that was favored by <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-waldrann-azennar/">King Waldrann Azennar</a> and often given as gifts. The creatures seemed to have grown but didn't exhibit predatory behavior, though they managed to charm Brush by accident, so the party left them some food and Jean tried to convey to them that they should stay inside the aviary.  
  
However, just after leaving the aviary, Brush suddenly stepped into a beartrap, locking him in place. In the distance, just next to the lake, a disemboweled triceratops was visible and as Jean and Nyla were still fiddling with the trap, a man suddenly stepped out of hiding. Within seconds, another Triceratops, <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">King Blackhorn</span> teleported next to him. After a brief exchange, it was revealed that the man, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/toront/">Toront</a> was the son of the Zookeeper who was held in the cage earlier, and had been feeding people to <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/old-gnarly/">Old Gnarly</a> on behalf of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a>. Even <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">King Blackhorn</span>'s wife, <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Queen Bonnyspike</span> the dead triceratops we saw, was killed by Old Gnarly. His teleportation ability probably also came from the rain.  
After convincing <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/toront/">Toront</a> that they indeed killed Old Gnarly already, he broke down and confessed to the party that deeper in the zoo are a group of <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/vegabonds/">Vegabonds</a> who had been sent by the queen to be fed to the crocodile. Jean seemed to have some reaction to that name, but did not elaborate further.  
<a class="wiki-link" href="/people/nardar-tyke/">Nardar Tyke</a> was apparently also one of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/vegabonds/">Vegabonds</a> and still held inside the zoo, so the party convinced <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/toront/">Toront</a> to bring them to him. On the way they passed a mimic cage and, edged on by the sign to find the mimic, Nyla stopped to look for it and indeed spotted it. However, as she tried to feed it, it instead attacked the party and a fight ensued while Nyla was still scolding it.
