The party woke up after a more or less restful night. Jean was apparently out as he saw the sun rise and returned home to find <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/rifa/">Rifa</a> and her son already having left, leaving a note behind which said they would contact him in a bit. Leon was hungover from the night before. Nyla woke up and since she didn't have enough time to go through her morning routine, Jean offered to do her hair, which she reluctantly agreed to, but kept a close eye to see if he would do it "right". In order to fit in at <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/king-waldranns-funeral-procession/">King Waldrann&#x27;s Funeral Procession</a>, Jean brings everyone to <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/jeans-apartment/">Jean&#x27;s Apartment</a> and gets Brush some better clothes. Nyla is very excited to be in the upper class district and by all the costumes he has. Jean gifts her a very gaudy top which she fell in love with.  
  
### The Funeral  
The party joined <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a> and the royals and nobles at the funeral in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/royal-mausoleum/">Royal Mausoleum</a> in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/royal-palace/">Royal Palace</a>. As they entered, Rozena stopped briefly, looking somber at the sarcophagus of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/former-queen-alyssa-azennar/">Former Queen Alyssa Azennar</a>. Jean received a a telepathic message from <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/jeans-ring-of-mindshielding/">Jean&#x27;s Ring of Mindshielding</a>. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/queen-lyrencia/">Queen Lyrencia</a> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a> were in attendance, and everything seemed to be going well for a moment, as the priests started reciting prayers. However, they were interrupted by the glaive on <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/former-prince-krasnar/">Former Prince Krasnar</a>'s statue starting to glow in an eerie light and flowing up in the air, as a voice called out at the dead <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-waldrann-azennar/">King Waldrann Azennar</a> again, calling him a traitor.

The party rushed to defend the royals and nobles in attendance, but all attacks against the animated glaive seemed to be doing remarkably less damage than they should. The glaive emitted a ghostly wave of energy, which frightened everyone, so Rozena took control of Nyla's body in her place. Meanwhile, Jean managed to shake it off and figured out that the glaive in the air was just a projection of the real one still on the statue. Together, he and Rozena destroyed the glaive and the projection disappeared. However Jean noticed that Nyla wasn't herself, and asked her about it. Rozena confirmed his suspicion, but did not give her name, only swearing him to keep the secret. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a> asked the party to meet him later before working on organizing an evacuation. The funeral ceremony was to be repeated in private.  
  
### Investigation  
Jean left the party to visit an informant and Nyla remained in charge of the rest, as she seemed the most responsible one. However, while she, still overwhelmed by the responsibility, turned to Rozena for coaching, Urgo and Brush started loudly conversing about the happenings, tarnishing both <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/queen-lyrencia/">Queen Lyrencia</a>'s and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a>'s names in front of them. While guards approached, Nyla barely managed to defuse the situation and ushered the party out of the place.  
Meanwhile, Jean changed their appearance and went to visit <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/tyx/">Tyx</a> in her house, presumably interrupting her teatime. Before letting him in, she asked to see "a coin" <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/jeans-mystery-coin/">Jean&#x27;s mystery coin</a>. There, jean learned of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/kidnapping-of-queen-lyrencia/">Kidnapping of Queen Lyrencia</a>, and the backstory which led to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/death-of-prince-krasnar/">Death of Prince Krasnar</a>. He found it suspicious how the death was framed to be due to quicksand.  
Brush went to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/lucky-leap-tavern/">Lucky Leap Tavern</a> to keep the appointment with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/gratus-ultio/">Gratus Ultio</a>, and Nyla, at the suggestion of Rozena, accompanied him. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/gratus-ultio/">Gratus Ultio</a> said that he, as a member of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/knights-of-the-crown/">Knights of the Crown</a> would have to attend a meeting soon, but wanted to keep his promise to meet Brush. However, he does not know more about Brush's origins, but said the name <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/harveston/">Harveston</a> (A city) comes to mind, and that one <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/father-gibbson/">Father Gibbson</a> might know more.  
When Jean came back to Urgo and Leon, and learned about what happened, he was flabbergasted, and as he confronted Urgo, he brought up the same conversation within earshot of the guards again, which led to them fleeing and coming to the tavern as well.  
After the party reconvened, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/gratus-ultio/">Gratus Ultio</a> gave some more background lore on <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/the-shining-light-and-the-darkness-within/">The Shining Light and The Darkness Within</a>, as well as the relation to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/dark-star/">Dark Star</a> and the <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/age-of-brightness/">Age of Brightness</a>. Nyla was officially welcomed into the party, while Brush and Urgo got reprimanded and punished by Jean.  
  
### The Unholy Coronation  
Keeping their promise, the party went to meet <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a> at his mansion. After a brief small talk, he revealed the reason for asking them to come: At <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-coronation-of-aphina/">The Coronation of Aphina</a>, he wanted the party to attend and help with security, as well as follow up investigations into what happened at <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/king-waldranns-funeral-procession/">King Waldrann&#x27;s Funeral Procession</a>, offering to pay them 500 gold pieces each. After a short discussion, considering their more hostile relationship with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a>, the party decided to accept, mostly as a favor to Monder. Lord Monder seemed to carry sadness and guilt for his failure to keep his oath.  
Before the coronation, the party had a bit of time to spend with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-kiryn/">Lord Kiryn</a>, who was very excited about his new pet. Nyla colored his hair blue to match the wolf's fur.  
The coronation in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/throne-room/">Throne Room</a> of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/royal-palace/">Royal Palace</a> was attended by the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/knights-of-the-crown/">Knights of the Crown</a>, many nobles, and various members of the clergy. Things went as expected until the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/crown-of-aglarion/">Crown of Aglarion</a> was placed on <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a>'s head. Suddenly, the sky turned <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/new-purple/">New Purple</a> and a violent storm began. She laughed as she proclaimed her rule, outlawing laughing, ponytails, and sandals.
