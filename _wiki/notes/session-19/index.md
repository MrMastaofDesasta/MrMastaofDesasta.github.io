#Notes 

The cursed rains hammered down upon the city with increased intensity, the darkness only interrupted by the streaks of thunderous lightning and the embers rising into the air from countless smaller fires. Nyla was running despite her tiredness, her mind desperately focused on getting to the nearest temple, the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/onadbyr-temple-of-the-eternal-mother/">Onadbyr Temple of the Eternal Mother</a>. For the first time since days ago, she did not even smell the sickly sweet scent of the rain as it whipped into her face, blurring her vision and soaking her hair. The only thing she felt acutely was the burning sensation on her palms which the ropes left behind when she tried to pull up the priests, acolytes, and temple servants. Meanwhile Urgo and Brush entered the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/lucky-leap-tavern/">Lucky Leap Tavern</a>, feeling the fire burn and crack their skin. Inside they found <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/ricio-tane/">Ricio Tane</a>, laying dead on the counter, and Brush rushed towards him, while Urgo ran upstairs, hearing the voice of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/evra/">Evra</a> and some growling. Jean reunited with Leon and, not knowing anything of what happened, they started walking and talking out of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/monster-menagerie/">Monster Menagerie</a> and into the streets of <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/onadbyr/">Onadbyr</a>. Only there did they notice the smell of smoke on the rain and, worried for the others, increased their pace. However, as they approached the tavern, Jean noticed a fiery glow on the sky, coming from the direction of <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/jeans-apartment/">Jean&#x27;s Apartment</a>, so they split off from Leon and started running towards there instead.

# The Flames Erasing History
While Brush tried to help Ricio, Urgo was running upstairs where he heard Evra's voice from. Behind a door, which he broke down, he found her, having grown to over twice her original size and wildly swinging a broadsword around, with a disemboweled  <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/were-hellhound/">Were-Hellhound</a> dead on the ground. After catching the sword, he eventually managed to calm her down and bring her back to the ground floor. However, Brush was unsuccessful healing Ricio, as he had already passed away, no matter how hard he tried and tried. Urgo sent Evra to wait outside, where she came across Leon and tried to get him to teach her magic now, but he went inside and down into the basement with Urgo.
There, they found <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a>, dead and pinned to the wall by a sword through his stomach. Surrounding him were several dead <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/were-hellhound/">Were-Hellhound</a>s. As they tried to retrieve him, a <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Key</span> fell from his hand, which Leon picked up. Inside the cellar were even more bodies, witness to the terrible battle that took place here, including <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/gratus-ultio/">Gratus Ultio</a> and several guards. Urgo picked up all the bodies he could find to bring them outside, while Leon investigated the cellar more. He heard scratches coming from a corner and found <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/jeagan/">Jeagan</a> behind a hidden door, seemingly unharmed but unresponsive. With her was <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/lord-tunalias-tibberton-rumlyn-the-fat/">Lord Tunalias Tibberton Rumlyn &#x27;The Fat&#x27;</a> and a ladder, on top of which Leon found a horse and all the party's pets , presumably evacuated by Jeagan before she succumbed to whatever befell her. On the way out, Urgo met Brush as he dragged Ricio's body out of the tavern.

Jean rushed towards their apartment, not paying attention to their surroundings even as the lightning crackling above head got louder and louder. Almost every house on their street seemed to carry some small flames, but none was engulfed like the tavern was. Even the house of Jean's own apartment had some fire on the door, but Jean was able to put it out before walking inside. Their apartment was not on fire, but covered in a thick layer of soot and ash. All the flames in the area seemed indeliberate, likely the result of someone passing by and spraying fire around as they were heading to the site of some violation. Frustrated by the attack, Jean considered just packing up and leaving Onadbyr, but eventually just gathered their things, particularly including various instruments and <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Jean&#x27;s Mysterious Documents</span>. They took around half an hour before finally arriving at the burning tavern, shocked to find the tragedy in front of their eyes. After exchanging a few hurried words with Evra, they rushed in, but encountered the others just on their way out, noting the bodies they were carrying but not realizing they were dead. 
While Urgo and Brush took them outside, Jean and Leon went back inside to head to the secret room, hoping to find more survivors there. As Jean asked about Jeagan and the others, Leon did not have the heart to tell them that they were all most likely dead. However, no one responded to their shouts, the only sound being the crackling of flames. They began by searching the armory, which had been almost cleaned out at that point, but suddenly Jean managed to hear a faint scratching noise and pulled open a door, where they found <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-finia/">Lady Finia</a> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-kiryn/">Lord Kiryn</a>, unconscious but breathing, hidden inside two barrels. They grabbed the two and carried them out of the building, but did not check the sleeping room.

# Strung up like discarded toys
Finally out of breath, Nyla arrived in front of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/onadbyr-temple-of-the-eternal-mother/">Onadbyr Temple of the Eternal Mother</a>, stumbling and falling to her knees on the lawn in front. She shivered, both from the cold, the desperation, and the fear of seeing what she knew she would find. However, despite Rozena's encouragement that she would not have to look, Nyla eventually raised her eyes.
At first, she saw the long scratches along the lawn, like claws ripping out chunks of it. Then she looked upon the altar, the one where she had placed an offering just days before, when the world still made sense. But just beyond that, she saw the charred, cracked doors to the temple. She clawed at the wood, pulling the doors open, but inside, only more death awaited. At least eight bodies had been strung up throughout the hall, their skin charred in places. Under tears, Nyla shot at the ropes of the closest one, the body falling to the ground with a thud, evoking more desperate apologies as she desperately tried to administer first aid to the corpse, while Rozena tried to comfort her. Finally, as a last resort, Nyla gathered the materials she saw used in Revivification Rituals and haphazardly arranged them, praying to all the gods she knew or anyone else who would listen. She begged for someone to help her, to revive at least this one person, but it was in vain.

Seeing that no one responded to her, the temple still filled with nothing but death, Nyla broke down and apologized over and over, while Rozena tried to assure here that it wasn't her fault.

Her mind dull from the repeated trauma and the hopelessness of the situation, Nyla stumbled to her feet and slowly went further inside, climbing the building to the roof from where she hoped to see the other temples. She hoped to see a sign of life from them, wishing for a glimmer of hope. However, as her thoughts were hazed and she was starting to dissociate, she did not immediately notice Rozena directing her towards one of the acolyte's beds, or register the message written in blood on the sheet.

Rozena mentioned that the message may be referring to <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Willow&#x27;s Creek</span>, a grove of druids. Not understanding what she meant, Nyla asked what that had to do with anything, and Rozena explained that maybe some of the clergy escaped there. Even though she was not in any kind of headspace to feel hope, Nyla still nodded to placate Rozena before continuing. From a window, she saw the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/onadbyr-temple-of-the-redeemer/">Onadbyr Temple of the Redeemer</a>, seemingly not on fire, but dark and without signs of life. Rozena barely managed to talk Nyla out of going there as well, citing the danger and begging her to stay. Leaning against the wall, Nyla expressed a desire to have died with the rest of the temples, which made Rozena incredibly sad and she told her not to say such things. In the end, Nyla retreated into her own mind and let Rozena take over, asking her to take her back to the party.

# Evening of Loss
As she arrived at the tavern wearing Nyla's body, Rozena bellowed towards Brush and Urgo to help her, before handing control back to her and disappearing. Nyla, suddenly conscious again, stumbled and fell to her knees, staring straight ahead into the flames without registering exactly what's going on.

<div class="callout callout-quote">
  <div class="callout-title">Rozena to Brush</div>
  <div class="callout-content">
HELP HER!
  </div>
</div>


Brush rushed forward to find out what was wrong, only to be greeted by Nyla's haunted, hollow eyes, as she whispered again and again that "Everyone is dead..." Unsure what to do, he suddenly hugged her, which broke Nyla out of her stupor, her eyes widening in surprise before she leaned in and clung to him, starting to cry and sob violently until she passed out a few minutes later.
When Jean and Leon came out of the building, they loaded up the bodies on a table which Leon made levitate and carried by Urgo, while Jean carried Nyla themselves, noticing how light her body was. Amaterasu came out to lay on her face to comfort her. Jean and Leon figured that <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Lord Monder&#x27;s Key</span> probably is the key to his mansion, but they decided to return to Jean's apartment for now. Carrying so many bodies, the trip takes a while, even though Jean attempts to use the back alleys which Nyla showed them.
Once snuck inside the apartment, Jean placed Nyla, Brush, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-finia/">Lady Finia</a>, and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-kiryn/">Lord Kiryn</a> in the bedroom, while they, Urgo, and Leon remained in the living room with the corpses of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/gratus-ultio/">Gratus Ultio</a>, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a>, and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/ricio-tane/">Ricio Tane</a>. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/jeagan/">Jeagan</a> seemed to still be alive, though she didn't respond to healing magic and her survival was unclear, being in an odd state. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/evra/">Evra</a> sat in a corner, oddly quiet for her.
Before bedtime, Jean made a quick dinner and left it open for anyone who would wake up hungry in the night. Brush made a quick painting of today's events before going to sleep. Leon received a message telling him that his parents are asking if he is okay. Jean plans to stay up the entire night to stand guard. The day comes to a close with everyone exhausted and tired, their minds bearing scars from loss and tragedy, some larger, some smaller, but none were spared.
