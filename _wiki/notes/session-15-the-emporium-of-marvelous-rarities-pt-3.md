The party fled the vault and made their way back through the Emporium. Brush paused at some point during the escape, stopping as though he heard something. He stumbled after the group and complained of not feeling well. They made it out of the shop without further incident.  
Outside, Urgo waited in the rain with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/evra/">Evra</a>, who had been keeping him company in her own way. She deemed him uninteresting until he showed her his mutated tentacle, after which she began listing "all the animals" to him while they waited.  
  
#### Back at the Lucky Leap  
At the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/lucky-leap-tavern/">Lucky Leap Tavern</a>, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/ricio-tane/">Ricio Tane</a> took one look at <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/evra/">Evra</a> and recognized her as his late wife who incurred <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/master-bertio-ranberd/">Master Bertio Ranberd</a>'s wrath years ago and hasn't been heard from since. He took her aside to speak with her alone. The party, meanwhile, exchanged what they had learned about <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/master-bertio-ranberd/">Master Bertio Ranberd</a>. It also came to light that Brush had lost some of his memory — though he had also apparently received some kind of warning about a vague approaching threat during his time in the vault.  
Ricio emerged grateful, and with a clear intent to pursue revenge against <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/master-bertio-ranberd/">Master Bertio Ranberd</a>. The party made plans to visit <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/nardar-tyke/">Nardar Tyke</a> the following day — the man who supposedly knew the location of the secret entrance into the palace.  
Before sleeping, Leon consulted his books, and Nyla took the time to identify her new <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Alchemy Jug</span>.  
  
#### The Next Morning  
Breakfast was warm and unhurried. Jean, noticing Brush was avoiding the healthier options, told him that new colors were hidden at the bottom of the bowls — successfully getting him to eat. When Brush made a move toward Nyla's food, she hissed at him and reached for a spell.  
The party said their goodbyes before heading out, including to Ricio, who had spent the night with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/evra/">Evra</a>. They had not gotten far through the streets when a terrified woman broke from the crowd, thrust a baby into Jean's arms, and ran.
