The party went upstairs and made sure everything else was clear. Leaving the boy (<a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-kiryn/">Lord Kiryn</a>) downstairs who ended up killing <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/eldon-lobo/">the monk</a> off. The party managed to figure out that revivify wasn't working on the mom (Of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/rumlyn-family/">Rumlyn Family</a>) because the monk's beard prevented healing. They cured her poison and were able to revive her though she remained unconscious. With the boy and mom saved they headed back up to their tents.  
  
During their search of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/royal-stud-farm/">Royal Stud Farm</a>, Jean had found letters between <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-rumlyn/">Lord Rumlyn</a> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a>. Lord Rumlyn trying to use coded language to explain susicious witch/<a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/cult-like-activities/">Cult-like Activities</a>. Lord Monder's response being "keep me updated". So the party was weary of whether they could trust Monder.  
  
As such, they decided to split. With <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/urgo/">Urgo</a> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/brush/">Brush</a> staying with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/jeagan/">Jeagan</a> and the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/rumlyn-family/">Rumlyn Family</a>, while <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/jean/">Jean</a> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/leon/">Leon</a> went to talk to Monder to figure out how much of this he was aware of.  
  
Leon and Jean initially claimed all the Rumlyns had died. But, seeing Monder's reaction, they did eventually let them know that the boy and mom were alive. Monder explained that he has an oath to the crown and he was hoping to get some evidence of these suspisions since it involved <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/the-countess/">The Countess</a> and accusing a royal could be punishable by death essentially. He thanked the party profusely and said he would home the rumlyns and organise clerics to assist. He did also ask them to keep this quiet for now. If the countess is involved and with some strange things happening - he wants time to try and figure things out.  
  
But said that they could meet in the morning to check on the mom and kid.
