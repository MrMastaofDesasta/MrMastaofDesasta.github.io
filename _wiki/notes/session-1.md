With the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/kings-games/">King&#x27;s Games</a> starting in a week the party headed to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/lucky-leap-tavern/">Lucky Leap Tavern</a> to unwind and join in some of the beginning festivities. they all met <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/ricio-tane/">Ricio Tane</a>, the owner of the tavern and ex-adventurer. The party seemed to get along well with them. <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/jean/">Jean</a> performed some songs. A small kid with bushy red hair approached <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/brush/">Brush</a> asking about his glow. During the interaction she had mentioned she was allowed to drink (lying). <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/jean/">Jean</a> rolled shit on insight and thought that the mom maybe let her have a sip or two of alcohol. <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/jean/">Jean</a> followed the mom out to talk to her about it. To catch up with her, he stole a goat. This is now the party goat - <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/fuck-ass-goat/">Fuck Ass Goat</a> (we do not use their initials). Turns out the mother just gave them apple juice and Jean gave them a lot of gold to help get them settled.
Back at the Tavern, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a> (one of the leaders of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/knights-of-the-crown/">Knights of the Crown</a>) came into the tavern (greeting Ricio his friend) and went to talk with a Halfing. <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/brush/">Brush</a> thought that this was the King and became enamoured and subsequently drew a picture of him. The party overheard <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/raffolk/">Raffolk</a> (the halfling) and Lord monder talking about a<a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/couatl-feather/">Couatl feather</a> which Monder said the King needed. They negotiated prices. Raffolk mentioning he was in debt and had a lot of kids and, therefore, couldnt go lower than 535 gold. Lord Monder left to go get the gold. After a short while, a huge bugbear called <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/gulfa/">Gulfa</a> walked in and started threatening Raffolk. Saying that if they didn't get their money (raffolk gambled at his establishment) they would kill him and the kids. As Gulfa walked away his hand knocked a lamp and started a small fire which set the feather alight. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/raffolk/">Raffolk</a>, distraught, got the attention and help of the party who agreed to help him get the second feather he has (his family inherited two) from his grandma <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/pearla/">Pearla</a> who doesn't like him and won't let him buy it unless vouched for. The party did try to stay with the halfling until Monder came back, but eventually were convinced to go with him to grandma's. They obtained a feather and paid 500 gold for it. On their way back to the tavern, Raffolk dissapeared in an ally and the party realised the feather was a fake.
While debating what to do, the party was greeted by Lord Monder who said they were informed they had been duped. They said it was likely the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/golden-masks/">Golden Masks</a> as they tend to disguise themselves and he hadn't been in the tavern at all nor did he know of this feather. During the discussion, he said that he would help them earn back their gold if they could go to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/royal-stud-farm/">Royal Stud Farm</a> and retrieve <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/miss-ivory/">Miss Ivory</a> (one of the horses) as they were needed for the procession. Monder seemed uncomfortable and as though they were hiding something. They did mention that their good friend, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-rumlyn/">Lord Rumlyn</a> was meant to bring Miss Ivory but that he hadn't heard from them. They party got some horses and headed that way. When they got to the top of the hill they saw the farm ablaze. After some effort and injury, they managed to save Miss Ivory and a few of the other Royal horses. Seeing some dead bodies and it being night time. They set up camp at the top of the hill in order to wait for the fire to die out and check it out in the morning.
