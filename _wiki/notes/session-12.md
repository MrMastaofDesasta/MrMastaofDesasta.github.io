The <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/tomb-of-master-arlen/">Tomb of Master Arlen</a> was silent and long undisturbed. The first room the party found held several broken golems, their bodies collapsed and scattered. Urgo took one of them with him as the group moved deeper in.  
  
A ceiling map depicted a <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Ziggurat</span> which was not shown on any other existing map of the kingdom. Nyla recognized the location though as she had been in the area before.  
  
The next chamber was large, containing a human skeleton, a furnace, and a number of martial golems that animated as the party entered. After defeating them, the party found the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/master-key/">Master Key</a> on the skeleton's remains.  
Nyla examined the furnace and discovered it was powered by a trapped fire elemental bound within its workings. She then descended a deep stairwell at the far end of the room, its walls covered in writings chronicling <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/master-arlen/">Master Arlen</a>'s life. Whatever rested at the bottom stirred at her approach, and the party fled before it could catch up.  
  
#### Return to Onadbyr  
  
Back in <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/onadbyr/">Onadbyr</a>, the party made plans to break into the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/emporium-of-marvelous-rarities/">Emporium of Marvelous Rarities</a>. The antimagic vault beneath it remains the only safe place to open the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/chest-of-magic-nullification/">Chest of Magic Nullification</a> and deal with the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/strange-idol/">Strange Idol</a> inside.
