As the night deepened and darkness fell, wrapping everything in a blanket of unknowing, the party was trying to find rest, and a measure of peace in <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/jeans-apartment/">Jean&#x27;s Apartment</a>. Brush laid awake, the experience of the day evoking vivid memories of an event from his past, him alone, in a tent, his legs burned, similar to how they were now. The feeling of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/ricio-tane/">Ricio</a>'s blood still lingered on his hands while he drifted off.  
Meanwhile Leon recalled the moment that Brush came stumbling out of the burning <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/lucky-leap-tavern/">Tavern</a>, carrying the body of Ricio, but in his dream, it almost seemed like the smoke was lingering, reaching out for him to pull him into the inferno. Eventually, Leon fell forward, into a pool of blood.  
Nyla on the other hand, did not dream of anything, as she only briefly awoke from her unconsciousness, long enough to feel <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Rozena</span>'s hand resting on her chest, before drifting off into sleep.  
Jean did not sleep, instead choosing to stand watch over the party. The night felt endless, stretching like thick honey smothering their thoughts, just like all days have felt recently. Their gaze wandered restlessly over their ruined apartment, where the layer of soot and ash covered their previous life, seeing <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Monder</a>'s hand gradually turn pale and eventually blue as the morning approached.  
  
# The Aftermath of Trauma  
The first to be up was <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-kiryn/">Kiryn</a>, suddenly standing in the doorframe to the living room. Jean immediately went to him and tried to distract him from the bodies and the death around. They did not have the heart to tell him that they were all dead, so when Kiryn tried to touch Monder's hand, Jean picked him up and brought him out of the room, saying that Monder fought so bravely that he has to rest for a long time now.  
Nyla awoke slowly, staring up at the unfamiliar ceiling for a bit while the memories of the last evening came back to her, and a numbness settled over her mind. Eventually, she pushed herself up and scanned the room, finding only <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-finia/">Lady Finia</a> next to her at first glance. Panicking, she looked for <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Rozena</span>, before finding her floating behind her, and sighing with relief. Rozena filled her in on what happened after Nyla passed out, then said that she would be going to the other temples to look. Nyla is initially hesitant, since Rozena is bound to her as an anchor, but eventually agrees, weakly promising that she will be okay by herself, but also asking Rozena to be careful in return. After a few more minutes, Nyla got up and wrapped herself in her temple robes, praying to <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/the-inevitable/">The Inevitable</a> to ask them to be kind to the souls of her temple family. She tried to remember every person, but got distraught when she reached the people who she never learned the names of, or hadn't interacted much. The thought of them going unmourned, of no one alive noticing their passing drove silent tears into her eyes.  
At the same time in the living room, Brush awoke and got up, realizing with a look around all the corpses, that the last night was indeed not a dream. He went up to the body of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/gratus-ultio/">Gratus Ultio</a> and tried to revive him, but the spell failed as he had been dead for too long already. He went down the line, body after body, but none of them responded to his magic. Even <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/jeagan/">Jeagan</a> seemed to have quietly passed away in the night. Eventually, Leon woke up as well and asked Brush what he was doing. Brush lamented that nothing he tried was working and that they were truly dead. Leon comforted him, saying that maybe Brush just needed some rest, but Jean intervened, their nerves clearly raw from the night, and bluntly said that they most likely couldn't be brought back.  
However, Kiryn overheard the conversation and was distraught, not understanding what was happening. Jean cursed their carelessness under their breath and moved to comfort Kiryn, explaining death to him. They ended by saying that Kiryn should remember the dead not as they died, but as they lived. Kiryn then walked up to Monder and quietly said his goodbyes to him.

While supporting Kiryn during his goodbye, Jean noticed a silvery spark in Lord Monder's left hand, but didn't investigate further to give Kiryn his moment. Kiryn then said goodbye to Jeagan as well, when Nyla finished her prayers, feeling resolve for just a second before the sadness settled back over her. She joined the others in the living room, just as Jean was carrying Kiryn back into the bedroom where they settled him on a chair and gave him one of their instruments to play with. Meanwhile Nyla silently went up to the bodies and started performing last rites and prayers for them as well. Her body moved almost on its own, the prayer seeming almost reflexive, rather than an active decision.  
When Jean returned, they wanted to discuss the events and the attack on the tavern, especially the possibility of a mole among the original group. Nyla stopped her prayers to turn around and revealed, her voice cracking and whispered, that <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Aphina</a>'s forces didn't only attack the tavern, but also murdered all the people in the temples. Before she started to cry again, she managed to describe the scene of their bodies hanging from the bridges and the ceiling of the temple she visited. The others were shocked at the news and went to comfort Nyla, eventually calming her down a little unsure if that would be what her temple family would have wanted. Jean's rallying cry to take revenge on Aphina resonated with Nyla and she agreed to go on, although she was a little unsure if that would be what her temple family would have wanted. In the end, the whole party decided to stick together despite the setback and to remain in <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/aglarion/">Aglarion</a>, seeing this through to the end even with the very real possibility of all of them dying. Jean made Brush and Nyla promise though to prioritize their own lives and not sacrifice themselves needlessly. Especially directed at Nyla though.  
After the conversation, Nyla continued praying, while Jean investigated the silver object in Monder's hand. It was a signet ring bearing the same initials as <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Lord Monder&#x27;s Key</span>, and with the engraved word "Crypt", followed by the numbers **2,4,2**. Meanwhile Nyla found that one of the guards body had translucent skin with dark veins, and its limbs were longer than usual. She didn't know what that meant though, so Jean came over to investigate as well. They identified the body as a <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Doppelganger</span> and found a golden coin in its pocket with a mask symbol, the sign of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/golden-masks/">Golden Masks</a>.  
  
# Lone Wolves  
Jean pocketed the coin without anyone noticing it, then got up and shared their findings. As the rest of the party was still wondering what this meant or how the doppelganger got there, they said that they may have a way of finding out and turned to leave. Brush and Leon tried to stop them, but Jean asked them to trust them and left, getting struck by <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Magical Lightning</span> on the way out.  
Nyla had turned away from the discussion and continued her work to distract herself from the mental image of her temple family hanging from the bridge, or the alternative - seeing herself digging her hands into Aphina's chest and slowly crushing her heart in bloody vengeance.  
  
> *Jeagan clung to the ladder leading out of the tavern, after maneuvering the pets outside of it, placing them next to a saddled horse in the hopes that the party would find them. Underneath her, in the room which the ladder connected to, she could hear arguing. Lord Monder, accusing some of his soldiers of betraying him. Then the fighting broke out, the air filling with the clanging sounds of metal hitting metal and the squelching of metal digging into flesh. Amidst the battle, one exclamation clearly reached Jeagan's ears. "Your thoughts betray you Lord Monder! We know about the journal you keep in the crypts!" She climbed further up, but felt her strength waning from the exertions of the evening. The tavern had simply grown too big of a hideout, too many people in one place. The queen's infernal soldiers had come in search of defectors and now traitors on top of that. Suddenly a columb of fire roared underneath her, consuming a figure with translucent, veiny skin who had reached inside after her. Then the flames reached her too, scalding her fingers and making her lose her grip. As Jeagan fell down the shaft, her last thoughts were dedicated to the fat cat in her arms. Then she hit the ground and a sharp pain stung in her spine, a jolt of fear streaking through her mind, before everything went dark.*

Meanwhile Nyla continues the rites and as she leans of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/jeagan/">Jeagan</a>, she hears a voice coming from herself, though not her own, saying "Sometimes death is necessary." Then she sees Jeagan's last moments as she was clinging to the ladder. Monder accused one of the knights of being a traitor and a fight break out. One of the doppelgangers mentioned that the know about the journal in the crypt. It seemed they had internal knowledge about monders thoughts, at least partially. They only knew that some people were hiding out here, not about the rebellion. Then a doppelganger was consumed by fire and Jeagan died. She tells this to the party who agree that they can't ignore something the enemies are after.  
- Nyla and Brush then finish the rites, and Nyla prepares to leave to retrieve her temple family's prayer implements, as Jean had suggested earlier. Brush tries to stop her but she tells him that while she appreciates the gesture, she will be safer on her own. He then draws a picture of the lighthouse by the north bay, which makes her tear up and she hugs him. She starts putting on her robes, then remembering that it is not good to go out in them right now, places them softly on the pile of her belongings, and turns invisible to leave.  
- Jean is flying over the city on their way to <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/trix/">Trix</a> to ask about the golden masks. On the way, they remember the skin with the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Strange Organization&#x27;s Tattoo</span> they have in their pocket and note the similarity to (SOMETHING). As they approach, they notice the vines and flowers overgrowing Trix's normally well maintained garden. The wooden door is starting to rot, and it takes just a moment too long before Jean hears a bump in the house, but no response. After calling out once more, Trix opens the door and greets them suspiciously, asking if they have been followed, but eventually lets them in. She seems stressed. Trix is preparing to leave Aglarion. During the conversation it is highly implied that their organization is in fact the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/golden-masks/">Golden Masks</a>, as they are referred to as "The Masks". Jean tells her about the attack on the tavern, and as they prepare to come clean about something, Trix asks if it is about "Jean" as they apparently have been using a fake identity to interact so far. Jean then pulls out the coin they found earlier and asks if they are in over their head and explains more about "Jean" and the party's plans to end the curse. After listening, Trix says that "they" work alone, though many work for the queen (but afterwards says that she doesn't know anyone who would work for the queen?). After Jean shows the skinpatch, she says she doesn't know about it. The queen is apparently hunting all knights that refused, which may be the reason for the attack on the tavern. She offers to get Finia and Kiryn out and to safety, if they can get them to her in the night, meeting point by the lighthouse. As they part, she warns Jean not to lie to her again and that she would be happy to take magical items as payment.  
- As Brush is playing with Kiryn, he hears a noise in the living room and goes to find Evra who asks if they are going to use their (the corpses) parts. Since that is how she was made and she is perfect. Brush considers it when she asks "Don't you want your friends back?". He offers to teach her magic and shows her a few spells.  
- Nyla sneaks out, barely sidestepping a lightning when she remembers her promise to live and come back unharmed. While there are too many holy symbols to take, most of them got destroyed, and Nyla has to sort through them to take the ones still intact. When Amaterasu tries to warm her hands, she gently pushes it back underneath he cloak. After collecting what she could and hiding the rest, she prayed to the dead people and promised to live for them and for the future they lost. However, as she did, she felt Amaterasu go limp. Panicking, she fell down and looked inside her cloak, seeing the body, but strangely got the feeling that it was right. As she hurried over to a roof, Amaterasu first turned into dust, then got revived into a small kitten. Nyla was confused but ultimately grateful that she didn't experience another loss. She turned back.  
- Jean returns to see Brush perform magic for Evra. They tell the others not to tell Nyla about this, but they suspect that the tavern was attacked because of all the knights and holy people they, in particular Nyla, have rescued and hidden. They exchange information and while they are making plans, Nyla returns and shares her vision of Jeagan with Jean. Jean shares some past exploits of Jeagan, highlighting her bravery (quote?) which makes Nyla cry again. They make the plan to investigate the Monder family crypt, then search the tavern for useful magical items. While the rest is squabbling over the incident with the kraken, Nyla walks to store the holy symbols, assemble some of them into her prayer bead necklace, and pack her bag again.
