#Notes

Time seemed to pass slowly in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/throne-room/">Throne Room</a> following <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/the-coronation-of-aphina/">The Coronation of Aphina</a>. The <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/crown-of-aglarion/">Crown of Aglarion</a> had formed <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Bone-like Protrusions</span> that dug into <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a>'s skin, causing <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Blue Vein-like Patterns</span> to spread across her face. Outside the windows, a storm had gathered, and <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Purple Lightning</span> flashed in sharp, violent bursts. Scores of soldiers were transformed into <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/were-hellhound/">Were-Hellhound</a>s — creatures of dark fur and broad shoulders, a flaming glow smoldering deep in their throats. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a>'s breathing turned raspy and labored. Then a window shattered, and the party was bathed in blood as the two guards posted outside <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a>'s mansion were cut down. Monder himself continued rushing toward the queen through the chaos.
In the violence that followed, the party acted alongside <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/gratus-ultio/">Gratus Ultio</a> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/father-golvin/">Father Golvin</a>, a head priest of the church of knowledge. Nyla slipped toward a cluster of priests, including <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/father-kalozark-solamyr/">Father Kalozark Solamyr</a> and began directing them to the exits, while Urgo and Brush forced the doors open and Jean retrieved the bodies of the two fallen guards. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/gratus-ultio/">Gratus Ultio</a> died shielding Brush from the <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/were-hellhound/">Were-Hellhound</a>s, who seemed to be targeting holy figures and users of divine magic with particular ferocity. As the party worked to clear the room, Brush attempted to reach Gratus Ultio's corpse but was stopped by Monder. Jean took the body instead, grabbing it and flying out through a broken window — and from above, he saw that the <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/cursed-rain/">Cursed Rain</a> seemed to weaken whatever it touched, and that vines in the same <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/new-purple/">New Purple</a> color Brush had drawn after his <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/nightmares/">Nightmares</a> were beginning to envelop the palace. Once most civilians had been ushered out, Nyla spotted <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/father-golvin/">Father Golvin</a> wounded and abandoned in the chaos. She turned them both invisible and slipped out.

### Escape from the Palace
Monder led the group outside and through a door that opened into the city's underbelly. Brush noticed something unsettling along the way — every bush and hedge bore flowers with <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/silvery-vein-like-embossments/">Silvery Vein-like Embossments</a>, their colors alien and deeply wrong. Before there was time to linger on it, hellhound guards came rushing in pursuit, and Jean arrived just in time to help the group push forward.
Once inside the sewers, the atmosphere pressed down heavily. Nyla suffered a panic attack in the dark and the group paused to rest before Monder urged them onward toward somewhere safer. <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/amaterasu/">Amaterasu</a> rejoined the party as they moved deeper through the familiar smell of the tunnels. Jean contracted the sewer plague, though Brush was able to heal him before it took hold.
The sewers had other surprises. Jean noticed a strange rat watching them, and moments later the group was rushed by an unusually intelligent pack that made off with one of Nyla's money pouches and Brush's sketchbook. Nyla and Brush recovered both by killing one of the rats. In what might be called an act of diplomacy, Brush gave the remaining rat a gold coin.
Jean contracted the sewer plague a second time just as the group neared an exit. Rain dripped down through a grate above them, and where it touched the ground, veins began to creep and grow beneath the surface. An unseen monster lurked nearby, but a cleric threw a coat over Brush to dim his light, and the group slipped past it. They emerged outside the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/lucky-leap-tavern/">Lucky Leap Tavern</a>, where Monder hurried everyone inside. As Nyla crossed the threshold, a bolt of what felt like <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Magical Lightning</span> struck her through the rain and she collapsed.
<a class="wiki-link" href="/people/ricio-tane/">Ricio Tane</a> let the group in without hesitation, activated some mechanism that sealed the building, and brought everyone down into the basement, where Jean healed Nyla. Ricio then revealed a secret room, but only after the party swore to keep it between them.

### The Strategy Room
Inside, they found <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-kiryn/">Lord Kiryn</a>, visibly shaken. The party calmed him down and Nyla, left with him for a moment, shared some of her candy. Meanwhile, Brush quietly revived <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/gratus-ultio/">Gratus Ultio</a>, and he and Jean moved through the wounded, healing where they could. When Ricio returned, he invited the party to join a strategy discussion.
The second room was lined wall to wall with maps and writing. Monder sat down with the same anxious quality the party had first observed in him during their initial meeting. He explained his suspicion that the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/crown-of-aglarion/">Crown of Aglarion</a> lay at the root of everything now unfolding, and traced the problem back to an event the public had never been told about, the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/kidnapping-of-queen-lyrencia/">Kidnapping of Queen Lyrencia</a>.
As Monder laid it out, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-rozena-von-vyne/">Lady Rozena von Vyne</a> listened carefully, and quietly turned one of her rings.

##### Memories of the Death of Prince Krasnar
<a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-dasmag/">King Dasmag</a>, the Satyr King, had taken <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/queen-lyrencia/">Queen Lyrencia</a> into the fey realm. King Waldrann assembled a rescue party: <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a>, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/prince-krasnar/">Prince Krasnar</a>, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/master-aiden/">Master Aiden</a> the former archmage of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/arcane-tower/">Arcane Tower</a>, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/father-lester/">Father Lester</a> a high-ranking priest of Knowledge, and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-waldrann-azennar/">King Waldrann Azennar</a> himself. Monder remembered little of his time in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/feywild/">Feywild</a>, only scattered impressions. What he did recall was that Waldrann challenged Dasmag directly, and that Dasmag, intrigued by his prowess, returned the queen. As part of the exchange, Dasmag bestowed upon the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/crown-of-aglarion/">Crown of Aglarion</a> a <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/blessing-of-plenty/">Blessing of Plenty</a>. On the journey home, Krasnar and Father Lester were supposedly swallowed by quicksand. Monder made it quietly but clearly apparent that he did not believe this to be the truth, though he said he could not elaborate further.

Rozena suggested finding a way to extract the rest of what Monder knew without asking him to speak it aloud. Jean offered to read his mind.
What Jean found there was guilt, and a thought that surfaced clearly: _I know what he did was wrong, but he was our king._ He saw a memory of Waldrann striking some kind of deal with Dasmag — the terms unknown even to Monder. In another scene, <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Strange Rains</span> fell, and Krasnar stood furious before his father:

Waldrann replied that Krasnar was not the rightful heir, and the two came to blows. The memory turned red. In a final fragment, Waldrann swore his companions to secrecy, and Father Lester refused. Waldrann's response was cold:

Then Jean was pushed out.
Monder went on to voice his suspicion that <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a> and Dasmag had cooperated in some way regarding the crown. He had written her off as a spoiled child, but her actions now suggested something far more calculated.

### A Path Forward

Monder outlined what he believed their options were. There were rumors — circulated by the uncanny vagabonds — of another heir to the throne. There might also be a way to cleanse the crown, possibly by going to Dasmag himself, though that would first require the Key to the Palace. Ricio mentioned that he knew of a thief who had broken into the tomb of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/master-arlen/">Master Arlen</a>, the first archmage of the age after the brightness, and supposedly left with a <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/master-key/">Master Key</a>.
There was also the matter of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/chest-of-magic-nullification/">Chest of Magic Nullification</a>, a container with the power to suppress a curse, which might be used on the crown. The problem was that it already held something else: a <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/strange-idol/">Strange Idol</a>. Ricio described opening the chest and seeing it as feeling like his insides were being pulled in all directions at once. His wife, a wizard who had studied at the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/emporium-of-marvelous-rarities/">Emporium of Marvelous Rarities</a>, had stolen the chest from the shop's private collection. Deep beneath the Emporium lay an antimagic vault, which would likely be safe enough to relocate the idol into — though accessing it would not be simple.
There was one more name: <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/nardar-tyke/">Nardar Tyke</a>, the only person who knew the entrances and exits of the tunnel leading into the royal quarters. He lived near <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/the-dawn-of-the-ages/">The Dawn of The Ages</a>, an antique shop, and was at risk — the queen's forces might already be moving toward him.
Before they adjourned, Monder brought the party into an adjoining room, also mapped and written over, but lined as well with weapons and magic items. He handed over the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/chest-of-magic-nullification/">Chest of Magic Nullification</a> and pointed them toward the location of the tomb.
