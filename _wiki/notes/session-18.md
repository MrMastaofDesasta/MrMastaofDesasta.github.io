The session started in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/monster-menagerie/">Monster Menagerie</a>, right after the fight with the Mimic and several Rust Monsters. By now, the rain had grown heavier, affecting even <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">King Blackhorn</span> who seemed just a bit slower in his movements. As <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Toront</span> was panicking again, Brush stepped up and cast calm emotions to take the edge off, which worked but as the adrenaline left his system he also became tired. Meanwhile, Nyla found a group of strange <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Multicolored Ant</span>s and picked one up to carry her hex. As she touched the creature, she felt a slimy substance but not thinking much of it stashed the ant away, feeling herself also becoming more sleepy by the second. However, as it has been a long day, no one, including her, thought much of it.
The party then continued onwards to the place where <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Toront</span> hid the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/vegabonds/">Vegabonds</a> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/nardar-tyke/">Nardar Tyke</a>, a storage room underneath the griffin enclosure ahead. As they crossed the corner, the party came across a group of slaughtered guards, done in by beasts or monsters from the looks of it. Nyla knelt down to offer a quick prayer for their souls to the Inevitable, stumbling to her feet when the party continued on, only to be suddenly attacked by a glowing owlbear (who would later be named <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Josiah Joseph</span> by Jean) with a thundering roar attack, sending her and <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Toront</span> a few steps back, holding their ears. <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Toront</span> was surprised to see him awake at this hour and mentioned that he may just need to calm down. So Jean stepped up with a lullaby to send it into a calm nap again. However, this same song also almost made <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Toront</span> and Nyla nod off until they were shaken awake. Leon heard some distant shouting from the city but was unable to pinpoint the exact location.
As the party snuck into the enclosure, which held a hurt Giant Scorpion (<span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Deathsting</span>) and two Griffins, Urgo, Brush, and Nyla suddenly got attacked by three Gricks, which poisoned Nyla. However, they were quickly defeated by everyone's combined efforts, and the party managed to sneak past <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Deathsting</span> who was distracted by the three bodies, and bribe the Griffins with some jerky.

# Meeting with the Vegabonds
Underneath the Griffin enclosure was a storage area in a cavern. As Nyla entered and knocked the doorframe to announce the party, a group of people, the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/vegabonds/">Vegabonds</a>, all with similar features approached and surrounded them, seeming hostile but wary. Now the party learned that they were a group of wandering musicians and all part of the same extended family, as Jean claimed to be a fan of theirs. While Jean's antics seemed to escalate the situation, Nyla in her tired state tried to smooth things over until <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/taris/">Taris</a>, an impressively large woman and the apparent leader of the group appeared. Nyla and Jean talked up the party as part of a rebellion against the Queen and convinced the Vegabonds to help out with some information in return for a small donation of a hundred gold coins which Nyla offered to them. Mentioning <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/nardar-tyke/">Nardar Tyke</a>'s name also seemed to have helped.
The party was led deeper into the cavern where they met <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/nardar-tyke/">Nardar Tyke</a> himself, a proud man, and very insistent on being an architect and not a musician, who instantly got offended by Jean's flippant attitude and refused to negotiate with them directly, instead turning to the rest of the party. He was quite happy, however, to draw up a map of the palace and the system of secret love tunnels, even pointing out some places of interest in the palace. Leon took the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Map of the Palace and Love Tunnels</span>. As they prepared to leave, each partymember was given a pouch of grain to offer to the Griffins above ground.
However, at the exit stood <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Toront</span>, crying and surrounded by the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/vegabonds/">Vegabonds</a> who looked like they were threatening him, but agreed to forgive him if he worked for them. Hearing this made Jean extremely angry and they intervened, which further escalated the situation, until a standoff occured which Jean ended by pulling a pin out of their cloak and throwing it on the ground before storming off.

# Grief
Outside, Jean was playing music for the owlbear who he dubbed <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Josiah Joseph</span>. He wanted to stay there for a moment and after a short discussion the party left. Leon snuck back invisibly, while Nyla separated from the others to go to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/onadbyr-temple-of-the-redeemer/">Onadbyr Temple of the Redeemer</a> and the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/onadbyr-temple-of-the-eternal-mother/">Onadbyr Temple of the Eternal Mother</a> in order to look after the other priests and temple servants who she hadn't seen in a while, wanting to make sure they were safe and hoping to spend just a little time and regain a bit of normalcy in these times.
