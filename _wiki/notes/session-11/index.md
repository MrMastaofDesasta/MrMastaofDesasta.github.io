#Notes 

Starting on the evening of the apocalypse, the party recovers from the events of the day. Jean and Nyla have a talk with the male resurrected guard, which Nyla extends as they talk about their experience and trauma with dying. In the morning, they plan on how to get to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/tomb-of-master-arlen/">Tomb of Master Arlen</a>. First they want to go by the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/partys-house/">Party&#x27;s House</a>, to retrieve their horses and pets. Jean helps Nyla get ready again, at which point she ends up telling them about her transition. Brush is given a cloak that makes him seem ordinary and masks his holy magic. On the street, the people seem afraid and keep to themselves, though technically they are out and about. Even shops are open. The sky is in weird colors. Dark Green Vines and alien flowers sprout. Jean pulls the party into a shoe shop to wait out a patrol, where they end up buying the entire stock of, now outlawed, shoes.
The party retrieved the pets and horses, but on the was out noticed thorns growing around the house, quickly crushing the hedge underneath. While the rest left the pets with Ricio, Nyla took Amaterasu with her. Shorty before reaching the tomb, the party got struck by <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Magical Lightning</span>, which spooked the horses until Nyla calmed them down speaking in Tongues.
After a short rest, they tried to enter the tomb which was discovered by Nyla and Rozena behind an illusion. However, inside the cave they encountered <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/eshkilrantossiazz/">Eshkilrantossiazz</a>, a green dragon trying to get into the tomb as well, claiming to have learned of the location in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/etherial-cloister/">Etherial Cloister</a>. After a short verbal confrontation, they negotiated a cooperation to open the trapped door, agreeing to split everything 50/50.
Inside the tomb was a large statue of Master Arlen with several robotic arms extruding from his back, as well as miniature models of:
    - <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Azure Bridge</span>, a bridge made of a single blue crystal.
    - <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Falcon Blade</span>, the magical sword of the monarch.
    - <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/royal-palace/">Royal Palace</a>, as seen in Onadbyr.
    - <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Colossus</span>, a golem made up of small intricate parts.
    - <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Fists of Azennar</span>, a tower with heavy siege engines on its top.
    - <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Dynadog</span>, a strange mechanical hound.
    - <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Daedal Wright</span>, a multifunction construction golem.
    - <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/arcane-tower/">Arcane Tower</a>, the figurative model of the building.
As well as a pile of clay tablets with all kinds of writing on them in front of one of the doors.
While the rest were exploring the room, Jean copied the contents of the clay tablets into Brush's sketchbook. Nyla found out that the models once contained magic, most likely illusion, that has since faded. Upon opening the door behind the tablets, Jean found the room filled to the brim with more tablets, as well as a skeleton laying over a desk on the far side. As he approached, a ghostly <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/master-arlen/">Master Arlen</a> and a golem appeared. Arlen dictated something after which the golem spat out another inscribed clay tablet onto the pile. Initially, the two did not seem to mind anyone, but as Jean approached the skeleton, the ghost suddenly screamed.
