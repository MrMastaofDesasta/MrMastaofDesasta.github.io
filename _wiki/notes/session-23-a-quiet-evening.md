The party gathered in the smoldering ruins of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/lucky-leap-tavern/">Lucky Leap Tavern</a>, after a long day which ended with their exploration of it. Though each of the members was weathered and tired, some physically and some mentally, Leon and Jean started to tally up the items they found (c.f. <a class="wiki-link" href="/notes/session-22-the-crypt-pt-2/">session 22</a>, but decided to take everything for now and decide later what to keep, so nothing useful would fall into enemy hands. As they started to pack up, Urgo looked outside the window and noticed the <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/cursed-rain/">Cursed Rain</a> seemingly letting up just a little, or at least not getting more intense for the first time since it started. Brush healed the remainder of Jean's injuries from their fall.  
Though the party wasn't followed or noticed on their return trip, as they were entering the apartment building, someone quickly locked their door, which caught Jean's attention. Afraid there might be a spy, or a <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/tattoo-of-the-queens-supporters/">supporter of the queen</a> in the building, they held Nyla back and asked her to investigate together while sending the others ahead. However, the two of them determined that there was only an <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">ordinary family</span> hiding behind the door, scared of confrontation and struggling to survive. Jean left some gold for them, and Nyla attempted to reach out to comfort or reassure them, but not knowing what to say turned back. Before joining the others, Jean asked Nyla not to tell the others what they did because they did not want to appear soft.  
Meanwhile, inside <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/jeans-apartment/">Jean&#x27;s Apartment</a>, Brush and Leon were confronted once again by <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/evra/">Evra</a> wanting to use "the parts" of the bodies still there so they wouldn't go to waste, and demanding to use magic. When Leon showed her the Minor Illusion spell, she appeared miffed at the simplicitly and replicated it perfectly before demanding more.  
When Jean and Nyla returned, Nyla went to sit in the corner and continued cleaning and repairing the holy symbols she recovered earlier that day, while Jean went to visit <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-finia/">Lady Finia</a> in the bedroom. They took <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-kiryn/">Kiryn</a> off her hands and brought him into the living room to make some coffee for everyone. Evra once again demonstrated her ability to not only see through invisibility but not even realize its presence and demanded to be taken along on the next adventure.  
As Nyla worked to keep her hands busy and her mind off her anxiety, she felt Rozena's presence closer, giving her a bit of reassurance and comfort. The cup of coffee that Jean placed next to her grew cold on the floor. Meanwhile, as Jean brought Finia her cup, Leon pranked Urgo with the illusion of a fish.  
Jean had a conversation with Finia and told her about the plan to get her and Kiryn to <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/tyx/">Tyx</a> and out of the city. She gratefully agreed but refused to take any money as she had her own, and they say goodbye for now.  
The party then gathered again to make a plan for the next day, which was to first go buy supplies and then assault the palace from the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Love Tunnels</span>. As they were distributing the key items, Jean proposed for Nyla to take the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/chest-of-magic-nullification/">Chest of Magic Nullification</a> when Rozena appeared behind her and advised her to agree.  
Nyla immediately jumped up, wide-eyed, and ran out the room, ending up in a brook closet where the two had a heartfelt reunion. Rozena tells Nyla that many from the other temples were apparently able to flee and sought refuge in <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Willow&#x27;s Creek</span>. In return, Nyla told her about her own experiences and askes for advice, especially about the mysterious voice she heard and vision she experienced when performing rites on <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/jeagan/">Jeagan</a> (c.f. <a class="wiki-link" href="/notes/session-20-the-purge-pt-2/">session 20</a>). Rozena seemed surprised and looked into Nyla's eyes, remarking that maybe "her power has found another", but said she did not know what it meant.  
After coming out of the closet, Nyla apologized for her sudden departure and awkwardly introduced Rozena and the party to each other, then let her take over her body. Jean especially immediately clocked Rozena's noble bearing and overwhelming charisma. Otherwise, the meeting goes about as well as could have been imagined, with her mostly ignoring Brush and Urgo's remarks Jean remarked that spirits like her are not usually "good" and asked why she helped Nyla so much, which Rozena refused to answer out of concern for Nyla's privacy. Before disappearing again, she made it clear to the party that she trusts them because Nyla trusts them, and that her only priority was Nyla's safety. Any help she would give the party would be a byproduct of helping Nyla.  
After Nyla came back to her body, the party concluded the planning session by looking at the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/map-of-the-palace-and-love-tunnels/">Map of the Palace and Love Tunnels</a> they retrieved. There was a gate with (one of) the most difficult lock(s) in existence, so the plan is to use the Master Key on that. Once inside, the party first wanted to look in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/throne-room/">Throne Room</a>, then <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Aphina</a>'s private quarters if the crown was not there. Nyla handed over the Master Key, though it found its way back to her shortly afterwards, and took the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/chest-of-magic-nullification/">Chest of Magic Nullification</a>  
Before going to sleep, Nyla read through <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/lord-monders-journal/">Lord Monder&#x27;s Journal</a>, which contained notes about the <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/feywild/">Feywild</a> and the happenings of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/kidnapping-of-queen-lyrencia/">Kidnapping of Queen Lyrencia</a> and circumstances of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/death-of-prince-krasnar/">Death of Prince Krasnar</a>. Following that, it contained records of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Monder</a>'s investigation into the <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/cult-like-activities/">Cult-like Activities</a> together with the former <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-rumlyn/">Lord Rumlyn</a>. Lastly, Nyla discovered a scribbled note.

Nyla was a bit disappointed as she was hoping the journal would contain something to help, but at least this sensitive information did not fall into anyone else's hands. She then performed her nightly prayers, asking for the safety of those that fled towards Willow's Creek and that this nightmare would end soon. One way or another.  
Brush tried to imagine a world without colors, following a hypothetical posed by Jean. At his nightly drawing, the <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/tree-with-three-eyes/">Tree with three eyes</a> emerged again. When he tried to contact whichever entity might be behind this by writing "Who are you?" in his book, the tree, which had reverted to the image of a normal tree, seemed to shift, but did not respond.  
During the night, when the party was fully asleep, they suddenly heard a loud crack of thunder booming through their dreams and felt like they were falling.
