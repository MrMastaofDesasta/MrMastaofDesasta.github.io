Still a bit stunned after suddenly receiving a child, the party was unsure what to do, until Jean yelled out orders to pursue the woman. Brush and Urgo rushed after her, but apart from a warning that she, the child, and we were about to die, they could not get anything out of her before the woman began turning into a <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/were-hellhound/">Were-Hellhound</a>. Meanwhile Jean and Nyla managed to determine that the baby (<span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Baby from Onadbyr</span>) was about 10 weeks old. Having heard that warning, the party decided to head back to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/lucky-leap-tavern/">Lucky Leap Tavern</a> first before continuing onwards. They also noticed that the <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/cursed-rain/">Cursed Rain</a> seems to have started rotting exposed wood, grain, and other organic materials, as well as starting to bloom <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Yellow Flowers</span>.
Back at the tavern, the party handed the child off to <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/jeagan/">Jeagan</a>, promising to bring back baby food on the way.

Again on the road, or perhaps it would be better to say the back alleys, Jean and Leon spotted a <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Rat with 6 legs and eyes and a glowing tail</span> going down into the sewers. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/nardar-tyke/">Nardar Tyke</a>'s house seemed unscathed, but nearby bushes have grown and were splattered with blood and intertwined with vines. Jean noticed a next door neighbor glancing through her curtains and headed off to interview her while Nyla snuck up to the house and unlocked the door. 
Brush took a closer look at the vines and almost saw eyes on them, similar to the dream about the <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/tree-with-an-eye/">Tree with an Eye</a>.
Even inside, the house was still in pristine condition but seemed empty. Urgo and Leon joined her in investigating.

As he approached the neighboring house, Jean noticed a particular tattoo on the woman's wrist, a <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/tattoo-of-the-queens-supporters/">Tattoo of the Queen&#x27;s Supporters</a>. No one knew what happened inside the house, but given that they came back with information, things they found inside the woman's drawer, and cleaning blood off their shirt, it was not hard to guess.

<a class="wiki-link" href="/people/nardar-tyke/">Nardar Tyke</a>'s house bore no traces of a fight or invasion. In fact, the only thing of interest was a pile of feces in the bedroom, and that the cupboard was cleaned out. Nyla was able to determine that the feces came from some kind of large plant eating <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Dinosaur</span>, like they are held in the nearby <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/monster-menagerie/">Monster Menagerie</a>. (We do not ask why Nyla knows how to distinguish different types of poop by smell.) Jean rejoined them and relayed some of the information they gained from the woman. Without any other clues, the party determined that the best place to start looking would be the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/monster-menagerie/">Monster Menagerie</a>, however keeping the possibility in mind that Nardar could have been a druid.

# A Menagerie of Monsters at the Monster Menagerie
The zoo was locked down and vines grew around the edges of the gate. Nyla was able to easily pick the lock and the party went inside the gift shopy which they promptly raided for dinosaur plushies and money. Nyla grabbed a triceratops plushie which <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/amaterasu/">Amaterasu</a> liked.

Further inside, the party encountered <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/old-gnarly/">Old Gnarly</a>, a crocodile that grew abnormally large from the rain and gained the ability to speak. <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/old-gnarly/">Old Gnarly</a> apparently killed all the other crocodiles and many people, judging by the blood and bodies around. Behind him, an elderly caretaker was trapped in a cage. While negotiating, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/old-gnarly/">Old Gnarly</a> revealed that he ate children too and enjoyed it, which made Nyla and Rozena flare with rage and prepare to attack him. They are however stopped by Jean who proposed a sneaky plan to free the caretaker. Nyla turned him invisible and together with Leon distracted Old Gnarly so Jean could rescue the man. During the fight, Nyla noticed someone watching them from a corner of the park. After finally defeating <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/old-gnarly/">Old Gnarly</a>, Nyla found a <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Two-headed and fire breathing frog</span> to transfer her hex to.
