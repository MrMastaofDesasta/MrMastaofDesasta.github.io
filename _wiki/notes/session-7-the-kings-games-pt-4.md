After the Leap of Death game (the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/kings-games/">King&#x27;s Games</a>), Urgo catches a shark for Brush to draw. The party is approached by <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-kiryn/">Lord Kiryn</a> and his mother, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lady-finia/">Lady Finia</a>. They were busy with the funeral for <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-rumlyn/">Lord Rumlyn</a>, so they couldn't join earlier. Kiryn seemed excited by all the buzz, as if the darkness of the last days finally granted him respite.  
  
### Pet Shopping  
After the Conversation with Kiryn and hearing about his new fascination with Manticores, the Party and the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/rumlyn-family/">Rumlyn Family</a> went to <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/the-sanctuary/">The Sanctuary</a>, where Jean tried to get the owner, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lyla/">Lyla</a>, to sell Kiryn a cat and say it is a manticore. Lyla instead explained that the store does not let customers pick out a pet like that. Instead, the pets inside the tent pick out their owners. Without listening much, Kiryn just ran in and after a while returned holding a blue wolf. The next to go in was Urgo, who returned with a Sapphire Otter, which he calls <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/eir-the-fishlover/">Eir the Fishlover</a>.  
While Brush walks in, Jean approached Nyla to chat, and probably to sound her out as the newest member of the group. Nyla revealed her association with the churches of the Redeemer and the Eternal Mother, but otherwise acted a bit evasive about her past. It satisfied Jean though.  
Brush inside the tent met a colorful dalmatian puppy, which he calls <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/paint/">Paint</a>. Afterwards, Nyla walked in, at first overwhelmed by the complete darkness, and being left by Rozena at the entrance. She found her courage by whispering a prayer to the Inevitable and continued onwards, until she met a solar kitten, which she calls <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/amaterasu/">Amaterasu</a>.  
Leon met an owlbear cub which he calls <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/nocte/">Nocte</a> and Jean met a displacer beast kitten which was attracted by his tune and which he calls <a class="wiki-link" href="/pets/cat/">Cat</a>.  
  
### Rest  
After visiting The Sanctuary, the party and the Rumlyns go rest a bit before continuing to the Battle of Champions. They have some nice food and watch Kiryn play with his new wolf. Nyla asks how the party and the family met and is very uncomfortable when she hears about the death of Kiryn's father.  
  
### The <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/battle-of-champions/">Battle of Champions</a>  
The arena, <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/the-dog-pit/">The Dog Pit</a>, was filled with hundreds of people. The price for participation is a magic item. Brush, Jean, and Leon gave a potion of healing, while Urgo gave a Dust of Dryness. Nyla entered using an unidentified magic item which she acquired through Rozena. In attendance were <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a>, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-waldrann-azennar/">King Waldrann Azennar</a>, and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a>, who kept mockingly yelling over the crowd. The announcer, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/jeffle-pebblefoot/">Jeffle Pebblefoot</a>, introduced the start, listing all the participants and the magic item they entered, being openly disappointed about the healing potions. Jean tried to get the people excited again by claiming they were divine artifacts.  
#### Brush vs Jean  
The first match was drawn and it is Brush against Jean. Even though Brush tried valiantly to resist and fight, Jean was able to paralyze him and slowly drain his strength using a combination of vicious mockery and beating him with his lute.  
#### Nyla vs Urgo  
The second match drawn, and once again it is two of the partymembers. Nyla against Urgo. While the others were busy betting on Urgo, Nyla entreated Rozena to take over for her, which she did. Seeing Nyla's sudden shift in personality, as Rozena's infamous charm and overbearing confidence emanates from her, Jean instead bets on Nyla, saying he has a feeling. The battle turned out less one sided than the one before, but Nyla(?)'s magic kept him frightened and away, so she could slowly whittle him down until she finished him off with her rapier. Urgo, upon being healed expressed a perhaps overly aggressive desire for a rematch, which startled Nyla.  
  
The next battle is Leon.
