A magic shop in <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/onadbyr/">Onadbyr</a>. The leader is <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/master-bertio-ranberd/">Master Bertio Ranberd</a>. They sponsored the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/kings-games/">King&#x27;s Games</a>.
