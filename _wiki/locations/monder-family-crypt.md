The personal crypt of the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Monder Family</span>, located close to their mansion in <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/onadbyr/">Onadbyr</a>.  
Following his death in the attack on the Lucky Leap Tavern, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a> brought the information to the party that he hid a <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/lord-monders-journal/">journal</a> inside, connected to the numbers **2,4,2** which are etched in his <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/monders-signet-ring/">signet ring</a>. However, the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/golden-masks/">Golden Masks</a> also learned of this and were looking for it.
