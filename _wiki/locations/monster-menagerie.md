A zoo in <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/onadbyr/">Onadbyr</a> which displayed all kinds of strange creatures. Due to <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/the-curse/">The Curse</a>, most animals became mutated, as notable examples being <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/old-gnarly/">Old Gnarly</a>, <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">King Blackhorn</span>, and <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Queen Bonnyspike</span>.  
  
<a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a> apparently used the place to get rid of rebels by forcing <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/toront/">Toront</a> to feed them to Old Gnarly.
