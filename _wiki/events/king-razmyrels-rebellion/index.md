#Event 

<span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Razmyrel Valsyr Melkar</span> and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/master-arlen/">Master Arlen</a> led the only successful rebellion against the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Eternal Light</span>. 

They grew up together in an orphanage of the order, but under assumed names.

They befriended <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Cygnus</span>, who was secretly a <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/couatl/">Couatl</a> sent by <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/the-shining-light-and-the-darkness-within/">The Shining Light and The Darkness Within</a> to monitor the <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/dark-star/">Dark Star</a>. Eventually it revealed itself and trained them.

Eventually they found <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Sarath</span>'s notes and found the location of <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Samkiel</span>'s prison, the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Ziggurat of the New Moon</span>. Inside they found the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Crystal of the Immortals</span> and after attuning to it gained unimaginable power, but kept it a secret from <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Cygnus</span>.

The rebellion itself started by fracturing the order. They joined forces with the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Drayl City-States</span> and received a large sum of money from <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">House Wyltammer</span>.
Eventually they defeated the Priest-King, but <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Arzatraxos</span>, a red <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Dragon</span> who became an inquisitor fled to the south and escaped.
