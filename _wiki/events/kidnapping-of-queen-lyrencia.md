<a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-dasmag/">King Dasmag</a>, the Satyr King, had taken <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/queen-lyrencia/">Queen Lyrencia</a> into the fey realm. King Waldrann assembled a rescue party: <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/lord-cyne-monder/">Lord Cyne Monder</a>, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/prince-krasnar/">Prince Krasnar</a>, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/master-aiden/">Master Aiden</a> the former archmage of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/arcane-tower/">Arcane Tower</a>, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/father-lester/">Father Lester</a> a high-ranking priest of Knowledge, and <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-waldrann-azennar/">King Waldrann Azennar</a> himself. Waldrann challenged Dasmag directly, and Dasmag, intrigued by his prowess, returned the queen. As part of the exchange, Dasmag bestowed upon the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/crown-of-aglarion/">Crown of Aglarion</a> a <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/blessing-of-plenty/">Blessing of Plenty</a>.

Waldrann struck some kind of deal with Dasmag — the terms unknown even to Monder. In another scene, <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Strange Rains</span> fell, and Krasnar stood furious before his father: 

<div class="callout callout-quote">
  <div class="callout-title">Prince Krasnar to King Waldrann</div>
  <div class="callout-content">
It is my right, I deserve the throne. 
  </div>
</div>


Waldrann replied that Krasnar was not the rightful heir, and the two came to blows. (<a class="wiki-link" href="/events/death-of-prince-krasnar/">Death of Prince Krasnar</a>) Waldrann swore his companions to secrecy, and Father Lester refused. Waldrann's response was cold:

  <div class="callout-content">
Then you can never return — and you can thank me for not killing you here.

</div>
