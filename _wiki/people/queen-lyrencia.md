The now former queen of <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/aglarion/">Aglarion</a>. Second wife of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-waldrann-azennar/">King Waldrann Azennar</a> and mother of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a>.  
She married Waldrann suspiciously soon after the death of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/former-queen-alyssa-azennar/">Former Queen Alyssa Azennar</a>, but people did not talk much to respect his grief.  
At some point the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/kidnapping-of-queen-lyrencia/">Kidnapping of Queen Lyrencia</a> took place when <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-dasmag/">King Dasmag</a> brought her into the <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/feywild/">Feywild</a>. The <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/death-of-prince-krasnar/">Death of Prince Krasnar</a> followed in the rescue operations when Waldrann probably murdered <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/former-prince-krasnar/">Former Prince Krasnar</a>.  
  
How much she has to do with <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/the-curse/">The Curse</a> and Aphina's plans is unknown, but she was standing next to Aphina while she unleashed it.
