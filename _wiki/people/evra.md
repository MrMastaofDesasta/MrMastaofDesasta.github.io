Previously the wife of <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/ricio-tane/">Ricio Tane</a> before she presumably got caught by <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/master-bertio-ranberd/">Master Bertio Ranberd</a> and killed. Now she is a flesh construct/doll and was kept in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/emporium-of-marvelous-rarities/">Emporium of Marvelous Rarities</a> before she was discovered and rescued by the party in <a class="wiki-link" href="/notes/session-14-the-emporium-of-marvelous-rarities-pt-2/">Session 14 (The Emporium of Marvelous Rarities pt 2)</a>.  
  
She is very curious and especially interested in magic. She does not seem to actively remember Ricio, but following the events of <a class="wiki-link" href="/notes/session-19-the-purge-pt-1/">Session 19 (The Purge pt 1)</a> seemed somber in response to his death.  
  
She is also the confirmed <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/best-character-ever/">Best Character Ever</a>.
