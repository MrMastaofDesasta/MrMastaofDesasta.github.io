The first archmage after the <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/age-of-brightness/">Age of Brightness</a>. Together with <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">King Razmyrel</span>, he participated in <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/king-razmyrels-rebellion/">King Razmyrel&#x27;s Rebellion</a> and overthrew the Order of <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Eternal Light</span>.  
  
Arlen the Constructor built a number of wonders using the knowledge he gained from the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Crystal of the Immortals</span> in the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Ziggurat of the New Moon</span>, including the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/arcane-tower/">Arcane Tower</a>, and the <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Azure Bridge</span>.  
  
After his death, he was entombed in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/tomb-of-master-arlen/">Tomb of Master Arlen</a>.
