#Character 

Lady Rozena von Vyne is a dead noblewoman of the kingdom of <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/aglarion/">Aglarion</a>. She is <a class="wiki-link" href="/party/nyla/">Nyla</a>'s Patron and somewhat of a mother figure.

Original family name is Von Vyrn

Personality:
- Very much an air of nobility
- Overbearing personality (owns any room she is in)
- quiet controlled rage when talking about her death
	- Also when Nyla is in danger/gets hurt
- sometimes stern but mostly gently with Nyla

Appearance:
- Wearing a long cloak that floats behind her
- stands with hands neatly palced on topof each other
- wearing rings, one a wedding band, other a ring with three rubies,
	- Right hand: emerald band speckled with gold (which was gifted by her niece), gold band with a crown which looks like nine thorns (gifted by husband)
- Long and striking red hair, pulled back with a pendant looking like my rapier

Backstory:
- Came from a small noble house near the Shandar Mountains. Small but long line
	- Historically involved in aiding the clerics of the eternal light in the age of brightness (aiding slavery and stuff)
	- Carries some shame for that
	- Those that were opposing it, eventually changed the name from "Von Vyrn" to "Von Vyne"
	- Family trained in martial arts and were latently magical (In particular her and her mother)
- Rozena spent quite some time negotiating with bigger noble houses
	- E.g. the Wyltammers
- Wanted to make her own name, didn't expect to mean anything, was invited to a banquet by a friend, that's where she met the former queen <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/former-queen-alyssa-azennar/">Former Queen Alyssa Azennar</a>
	- Her friendship with the queen made her name
- Met husband Naxryn who is an Eladrin on a trip back home
	- Fairly powerful druid
	- He did not talk much about his past, but she does not remember much about it either
		- Not remembering bugs her
	- She does not know what happened but assumes he is dead.
	- But she remembers details of their life together.
- She came back as a spirit at the Ruins of the Royal Summer Palace
	- Rumors of another spirit that resides there, but she did not interact much.
	- Not where she thinks she would have been left unless someone would know
- Has no memory up to then
	- In the period just before she died, she has memories of very mundane things, feeling a bit modified/placed
- Attended a bunch of gatherings in order to learn more, but with less than great success. One of such was where she met Nyla
- Her Family and husband used to call her Rose, but most people, such as the queen, called her Zena
	- Hence the rose themes in her everything
- She is not sure how long she'd been dead, but assumes it's been a while (She is only now counting)
- Quite upset that there is almost no mention of her anymore anywhere. Or proof of her existence
- Nyla and her found her weapon in the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/thornwood-woods/">Thornwood Woods</a> where she and her husband had a little retreat. He liked the spot, though she does not remember why.
	- Rozena was initially hesitant to go but also curious.
	- We saw a thick layer of fog along with various traps which seemed natural but also placed
	- The retreat was still there, built into the ground almost like a bunker. At night, Nyla was attacked by a gnome. It didn't feel random. The gnome was wearing a <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Thorned Crown</span> which didn't look dissimilar to Rozena's ring. Upon his death we heard howls and wolves came attacking. Nyla barricaded herself and waited the attack out for a long time. The wolves kept at it for at least 48 hours, there were noises of digging and a scream in the distance. That's when Rozena told her where to find the weapon to get out.
		- Rozena sometimes still seems to think about the gnome and the crown.


Relations:
