The King of <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/aglarion/">Aglarion</a>. Famous for his martial prowess and generally beloved by the populace.  
Previously he was married to <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/former-queen-alyssa-azennar/">Former Queen Alyssa Azennar</a> and had a son, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/former-prince-krasnar/">Former Prince Krasnar</a>. However, following the death of Alyssa, he quickly remarried <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/queen-lyrencia/">Queen Lyrencia</a> with whom he had a daughter, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a>.  
When he went to rescue Lyrencia from <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-dasmag/">King Dasmag</a>'s <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/kidnapping-of-queen-lyrencia/">kidnapping</a>, the two kings sparred and Dasmag bestowed a <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/blessing-of-plenty/">Blessing of Plenty</a> on the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/crown-of-aglarion/">Crown of Aglarion</a>.  
However, on the way back, Waldrann <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/death-of-prince-krasnar/">killed</a> Krasnar who confronted him about changing the inheritance, and covered it up as an accident.  
  
Ultimately, Waldrann himself was <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/murder-of-king-waldrann/">killed</a> during the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/battle-of-champions/">Battle of Champions</a> by an <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/erinyes/">Erinyes</a> resembling Alyssa, who accused him of murder.  
  
Current Status: Dead
