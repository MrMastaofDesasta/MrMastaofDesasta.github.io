Former high ranking priest of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/church-of-knowledge/">Church of Knowledge</a>. Went with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-waldrann-azennar/">King Waldrann Azennar</a> into the <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/feywild/">Feywild</a> after the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/kidnapping-of-queen-lyrencia/">Kidnapping of Queen Lyrencia</a>. 
Following the <a class="wiki-link" href="/events/death-of-prince-krasnar/">Death of Prince Krasnar</a>, he refused to participate in the cover up and was subsequently banished by Waldrann. He probably remained in the Feywild. Current Fate unknown.
