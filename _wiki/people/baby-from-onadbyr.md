A young toddler the party received from an unknown woman in <a class="wiki-link" href="/notes/session-16-the-monster-menagerie-pt-1/">Session 16 (The Monster Menagerie pt 1)</a>. Shortly after handing them over, the woman turned into a <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/were-hellhound/">Were-Hellhound</a>. Following the events of the purge in <a class="wiki-link" href="/notes/session-19-the-purge-pt-1/">Session 19 (The Purge pt 1)</a>, their fate is unknown as their body was never found in the remains of the <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/lucky-leap-tavern/">Lucky Leap Tavern</a>.
