Was raised in <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/the-little-home/">The Little Home</a> orphanage in a town by the coast. Considers Miss <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Loryn von Val</span> a mother figure but left shortly after the orphanage closed down, despite Loryn offering to house her with the other children. She did this because she saw how much Loryn was struggling and out of a profound feeling of disconnect and non-belonging.

Nyla is suspicious of <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/house-gorso/">House Gorso</a> for their role in the closing of the orphanage.

<span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Charlotte</span>, who works for the Wyltammers helped set up a contact for the transition. Nyla's patron though is suspicious of <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">House Wyltammer</span>.

Transition was performed with the help of <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Kaia</span>, a druid from <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Willow&#x27;s Creek</span>.

Nyla feels indebted to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/church-of-the-eternal-mother/">Church of the Eternal Mother</a> and the <a class="wiki-link" href="/organizations/church-of-the-redeemer/">Church of the Redeemer</a> for giving her a new body. She respects the Redeemer for giving her life but considers them as having made a mistake with her original body. Forgiveable though. The Eternal Mother was the one who ended up "getting it right". Due to her brief time being dead, she feels somewhat close to the Inevitable and is grateful to them for having allowed her to return to life. If she can she honors them in shrines and temples. 

In the Church of the Redeemer, she worked a lot with <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/mia/">Mia</a>, a cleric there
