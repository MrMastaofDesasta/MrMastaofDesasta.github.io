An enchantment on the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/crown-of-aglarion/">Crown of Aglarion</a> bestowed by <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-dasmag/">King Dasmag</a>. Presumably the reason that <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/aglarion/">Aglarion</a> could enjoy the prosperity it did.  
  
Working theory since <a class="wiki-link" href="/notes/session-10-aphinas-betrayal-pt-3/">Session 10 (Aphina&#x27;s Betrayal pt 3)</a> is that <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/princess-aphina/">Princess Aphina</a> either corrupted the blessing into <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/the-curse/">The Curse</a> or that King Dasmag intended it as a trap from the beginning.
