The curse that is currently plaguing <a class="wiki-link" href="/locations/aglarion/">Aglarion</a>.  
It seems to be connected to the <a class="wiki-link" href="/lore/blessing-of-plenty/">Blessing of Plenty</a> which <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/king-dasmag/">King Dasmag</a> put on the <a class="wiki-link" href="/objects/crown-of-aglarion/">Crown of Aglarion</a>.  
  
Symptoms of the Curse seem to be <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/cursed-rain/">Cursed Rain</a> and weird colors, in particular <a class="wiki-link" href="/mysteries/new-purple/">New Purple</a>. The rain grows strange flowers, e.g. <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Yellow Flowers</span> and possibly also <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Black Flowers</span> though these seem to be connected to death.  
The rain also mutates animals, as seen in the Monster Menagerie, giving them magical powers and sometimes sentience. Examples are: <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Multicolored Ant</span>, <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">Two-headed and fire breathing frog</span>, <a class="wiki-link" href="/people/old-gnarly/">Old Gnarly</a>, <span class="wiki-unresolved" title="No page exists for this reference">King Blackhorn</span>. Though these mutants seem to have a higher resistance, the rain drains the energy of living beings.  
It also rots organic materials.
